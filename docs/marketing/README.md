# Drivable Marketing Docs

Planning and copy resources for controlled first-tester outreach and early launch preparation. These documents do not authorize public launch, paid traffic, or unreviewed customer delivery.

## Documents

- `DRIVABLE_FIRST_TESTER_OUTREACH_V1.md` - Who to recruit first, outreach messages, test observation, and follow-up questions.
- `DRIVABLE_AD_ANGLES_V1.md` - Twelve customer-problem ad angles with hooks, CTAs, audiences, and trust notes.
- `DRIVABLE_SHORT_VIDEO_SCRIPTS_V1.md` - Twelve 15-to-30-second video concepts for roadside, repair, buyer, seller, and report value.
- `DRIVABLE_BEFORE_AFTER_SCENARIOS_V1.md` - Ten customer stories showing the move from confusion to a clearer next action.
- `DRIVABLE_TRUST_CAPTIONS_V1.md` - Thirty short captions for responsible confidence, evidence, review, and inspection language.
- `DRIVABLE_LOCAL_LAUNCH_PLAN_V1.md` - Staged first 10, 25, and 50 tester plan with measurement and stop conditions.
- `DRIVABLE_ONE_PAGE_POSITIONING_V1.md` - Concise market, solution, offer, trust, moat, and launch-wedge positioning.
- `DRIVABLE_WEBSITE_COPY_BLOCKS_V1.md` - Reusable hero, report, scenario, trust, CTA, and FAQ website copy.

## Shared Boundary

Drivable provides informational, confidence-rated guidance based on information provided. It is not a certified inspection, confirmed diagnosis, safety clearance, emergency service, or guarantee of repair, sale, purchase, title, or legal outcome.
