# Drivable Short Video Scripts V1

## Production Rules

- Target length: 15 to 30 seconds.
- Show the customer's decision, not a magical diagnosis.
- Never film while driving or encourage symptom recreation.
- Add visible trust language where safety, buying, or repair decisions appear.

## 1. Roadside: "What Now?"

**Hook:** "The car feels wrong. You pulled over. What now?"

**Scene idea:** Parked vehicle, hazards visible, close-up of phone showing a simple evidence checklist.

**Voiceover/text:** "Drivable helps organize what happened, warning lights, risk signals, and the next questions. Stop, roadside help, tow, or inspection may be the next path - based on the information available."

**CTA:** Get a clearer roadside next move

**Warning/safety note:** Already stopped? Stay safe. Drivable is not emergency dispatch and does not declare a vehicle safe to drive.

## 2. Roadside: Ask Before You Tow

**Hook:** "Do I need a tow, or am I missing one useful check?"

**Scene idea:** Split screen: tow icon, safe parking icon, warning-light photo.

**Voiceover/text:** "If you are already in a safe place, Drivable can organize the symptoms and missing evidence before the next call."

**CTA:** Review the situation

**Warning/safety note:** Do not delay towing or emergency help when brakes, steering, smoke, fire, fuel, overheating, or unsafe location are involved.

## 3. Repair vs Sell Panic

**Hook:** "Another repair estimate. Is this still worth it?"

**Scene idea:** Estimate on a table, older car outside, customer comparing two cards: Fix and Sell As-Is.

**Voiceover/text:** "Drivable turns the vehicle problem into decision paths: inspect, repair, wait, sell as-is, or walk away from more spending."

**CTA:** Compare repair versus sell

**Warning/safety note:** Costs and outcomes are not guaranteed. High-cost decisions may require inspection and human review.

## 4. Used Car Buyer Hesitation

**Hook:** "That cheap used car is three hours away. Worth the trip?"

**Scene idea:** Marketplace listing, incomplete photo set, map distance, checklist overlay.

**Voiceover/text:** "Submit the listing, seller claims, photos, and your questions. Drivable highlights missing proof, red flags, and what an independent inspection should focus on."

**CTA:** Review before you chase it

**Warning/safety note:** Not a pre-purchase inspection. Verify ownership and title before paying.

## 5. Dashboard Warning Light

**Hook:** "A dashboard light just changed your whole day."

**Scene idea:** Parked-car dashboard photo, then on-screen prompts for exact light, symptoms, and codes.

**Voiceover/text:** "Do not guess from the icon alone. Drivable combines the light with timing, symptoms, vehicle details, and available scan information."

**CTA:** Organize the warning-light clues

**Warning/safety note:** Critical warnings, smoke, overheating, braking changes, or loss of power require prompt in-person help.

## 6. Mechanic Script

**Hook:** "Not sure what to say when the shop answers?"

**Scene idea:** Customer staring at phone, then reading a concise mechanic script.

**Voiceover/text:** "Drivable turns 'it sounds weird' into what happens, when it happens, what changed, and what you want verified before approving parts."

**CTA:** Build a better shop conversation

**Warning/safety note:** A mechanic script improves communication; it is not a diagnosis or repair order.

## 7. Seller As-Is Clarity

**Hook:** "Selling it as-is does not mean saying nothing."

**Scene idea:** Messy listing rewritten into known issues, repairs, evidence, and buyer questions.

**Voiceover/text:** "Drivable helps organize what you know, what you do not know, and how to describe the vehicle more clearly."

**CTA:** Prepare an as-is listing

**Warning/safety note:** Sellers remain responsible for truthful disclosures, ownership, title, and the transaction.

## 8. Weird Noise

**Hook:** "Tick? Knock? Squeal? Hum? The timing is the clue."

**Scene idea:** Text labels appear beside parked-car audio waveform and symptom-timing prompts.

**Voiceover/text:** "Share a safe recording and tell Drivable when the noise happens. Get possible paths, confidence, and what still needs hands-on testing."

**CTA:** Explain the noise clearly

**Warning/safety note:** Do not drive or recreate a symptom for a recording. Audio alone cannot confirm the cause.

## 9. Vibration

**Hook:** "Vibration while braking is different from vibration at idle."

**Scene idea:** Three static cards: braking, highway speed, idle.

**Voiceover/text:** "Drivable asks where you feel it, when it starts, and what warning signs come with it - then organizes the next inspection path."

**CTA:** Map the vibration

**Warning/safety note:** Severe shaking, steering changes, braking changes, or wheel/tire concerns require in-person help.

## 10. Before Wasting Repair Money

**Hook:** "Before replacing another part, ask: what was actually tested?"

**Scene idea:** Parts boxes stack up, then switch to evidence, confidence, and missing-check cards.

**Voiceover/text:** "Drivable helps you ask what supports the recommendation, what is still uncertain, and what should be verified next."

**CTA:** Review the repair decision

**Warning/safety note:** Drivable does not guarantee a cheaper repair or replace qualified testing.

## 11. First Look Report

**Hook:** "You may not need every answer. You need the next useful answer."

**Scene idea:** Quick scroll through summary, possible causes, confidence, missing evidence, and next questions.

**Voiceover/text:** "A Drivable First Look Report organizes the information you provided into a clearer starting point."

**CTA:** See a sample First Look

**Warning/safety note:** Informational, confidence-rated guidance. Not a confirmed diagnosis or inspection.

## 12. Outcome Learning

**Hook:** "The most important question comes later: what actually happened?"

**Scene idea:** Timeline from intake to report to repair invoice to feedback.

**Voiceover/text:** "Drivable follows the recommendation to the customer's action, actual cause, repair, cost, and whether the advice helped. That is how future guidance can improve responsibly."

**CTA:** See the Drivable learning loop

**Warning/safety note:** Customer reports and verified repair evidence must remain clearly separated. More data does not justify unsafe certainty.
