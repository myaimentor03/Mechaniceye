# Drivable Public Profile and Channel Identity Kit V1

This kit defines how **Drivable by Mechanic's Eye** should appear across public profiles. It does not authorize account creation, handle purchase, domain purchase, verification payment, publishing, or access sharing.

## 1. Identity Hierarchy

**Display name:** Drivable by Mechanic's Eye  
**Short display name when space is limited:** Drivable  
**Category:** Vehicle decision-support software / automotive education, using the closest accurate platform category  
**Current availability statement:** Drivable Check and Buyer Check — closed beta in preparation  
**Primary CTA:** Join the beta waitlist, only after the route is live and tested

Do not select platform categories or profile labels that imply a repair shop, inspection station, emergency service, insurer, vehicle dealer, lender, certification authority, or live marketplace unless Drivable actually operates in that role.

## 2. Handle Strategy

Choose a handle only after checking trademark, impersonation risk, domain alignment, and availability across priority channels.

Preference order:

1. `@drivable`
2. `@getdrivable`
3. `@drivableapp`
4. `@drivablebyme`
5. A consistent approved alternative

Avoid:

- adding an arbitrary year or region that will age poorly;
- using `official` unless needed for impersonation control;
- using `AImechanic`, `diagnosemycar`, `certified`, or `safe`;
- different handles on every platform when a consistent option is available;
- buying a handle or domain from an unknown party without ownership, transfer, security, and legal review.

Record selected and defensive handles in the account inventory. Company-controlled email, recovery method, two-factor authentication, and at least two authorized administrators are required.

## 12. Account Change Control

Record every change to:

- display name or handle;
- profile image or banner;
- bio, category, or links;
- administrator, agency, or recovery access;
- connected advertising or analytics account;
- payment method;
- automated publishing, messaging, or moderation tool.

The record must identify the requester, approver, operator, date, old value, new value, purpose, and rollback method.
