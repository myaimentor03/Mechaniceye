# Drivable Beta Recruitment Campaign V1

**Campaign name:** A Clearer Next Move  
**Stage:** Closed beta recruitment  
**Primary objective:** Recruit appropriate testers for controlled, non-emergency vehicle decisions.  
**Primary CTA:** Apply to test Drivable  
**Brand line:** Drivable by Mechanic's Eye

## 1. Campaign Promise

Vehicle problems rarely arrive with a clear explanation. Drivable is being built to help ordinary people organize what happened, what evidence they have, what remains uncertain, and what questions to ask next.

The beta is for learning whether that experience is understandable, useful, and responsibly cautious. It is not a diagnosis service, safety clearance, emergency service, or substitute for hands-on inspection.

## 2. Ideal Beta Participants

- Adults with a non-emergency vehicle symptom or decision.
- Owners preparing for a mechanic conversation.
- Owners deciding whether to investigate, repair, monitor, or sell.
- Used-car buyers willing to evaluate a listing without treating the result as an inspection.
- Sellers willing to describe known issues honestly and distinguish claims from evidence.
- Participants who can provide feedback and, optionally, a later outcome.

Do not recruit someone whose immediate situation involves fire, smoke, fuel odor/leak, severe overheating, brake or steering failure, severe shaking/loss of control, wheel/tire separation concern, an unsafe location, or another emergency. Direct them to emergency, roadside, towing, or qualified in-person assistance as appropriate.

### Why Participate

**Headline:** You do not need to know car terminology.

**Body:** We want to learn whether Drivable helps everyday owners, buyers, and sellers describe a situation more clearly, understand uncertainty, and prepare for a better next conversation.

**Benefits:**

- Guided questions about what happened and when.
- Help organizing written observations, timing, dashboard details, recent work, and user-entered OBD information supported by the release.
- A clear view of possible causes when the evidence supports them.
- Missing information and confidence explained in plain language.
- Useful questions for a mechanic, seller, buyer, or inspector.
- A direct way to tell us what was confusing or helpful.

### What Participation Involves

1. Complete a short application and choose the decision you need help with.
2. Share vehicle details and evidence you can collect without taking a safety risk.
3. Review the beta output and its limitations.
4. Tell us what made sense, what did not, and what you did next if you choose.

### Beta Boundaries

- Drivable does not declare a vehicle safe to drive.
- A possible cause is not a confirmed diagnosis.
- High-risk or unclear situations may be stopped, redirected, or held for human review.
- Buyer Check does not verify a seller, title, mileage, vehicle condition, or transaction.
- Seller statements remain seller-reported unless independently verified.
- Features, availability, and turnaround may change during beta.

## 4. Application Form Copy

### Required Fields

- Name.
- Email or approved contact method.
- General location (city/region only unless more is operationally required).
- Relationship to vehicle: owner/operator, buyer, seller, helper, or professional.
- Vehicle year, make, and model; VIN optional and not presumed verified.
- What are you trying to decide?
- What changed or concerns you?
- Are any critical safety signals present?
- What evidence is available?
- Can you participate in a short feedback follow-up?
- Required acknowledgments and consent.

### Safety Screen

**Question:** Is the vehicle currently smoking, on fire, leaking or strongly smelling of fuel, severely overheating, losing braking or steering control, shaking violently, showing a possible wheel/tire separation problem, or stopped in an unsafe location?

If yes:

**Message:** This situation is outside the closed beta intake. Do not collect more media or wait for Drivable. Move to safety if you can do so without additional risk and contact emergency, roadside, towing, or qualified in-person assistance as appropriate.

### Required Acknowledgment

> I understand that Drivable beta guidance is informational and based on the information I provide. It is not a physical inspection, confirmed diagnosis, emergency service, or safety clearance. I will not take a safety risk to collect evidence or delay qualified help while waiting for a response.

### Optional Learning Consent

Keep service delivery consent separate from optional permission to use de-identified data for evaluation or improvement. Marketing/testimonial permission must be a third, separate choice and must not be required to receive the beta experience.

## 8. Thirty-Second Beta Video Script

**Format:** Founder-led, natural setting, real parked vehicle, real phone/product capture.  
**Do not:** Film while driving, imply a live diagnosis, or show unapproved personal evidence.

**0–4 seconds — Founder near a parked everyday vehicle**  
“When something changes with your car, the hardest part is often knowing what matters.”

**4–10 seconds — Close details: parked vehicle; written timing notes; generic repair paperwork with all sensitive information hidden**

**19–25 seconds — Founder speaking directly**  
“It does not replace a mechanic or tell you a vehicle is safe. It is designed to make uncertainty clearer.”

**25–30 seconds — Simple brand card**  
“Apply to join the closed beta and help us build it responsibly.”

**End card:** Drivable | Closed beta in preparation | `getdrivable.com`

## 9. Creative Brief for the Recruitment Visual Set

### Route A — The Parked-Driver Moment

- Person seated in a safely parked, ordinary vehicle.
- Phone used only while the vehicle is fully parked; no capture or driving behavior.
- Natural overcast or late-afternoon light.
- Expression: concerned but composed, not frightened.
- Wide negative space for editable headline.
- No visible brand logos, impossible dashboard indicators, or embedded text.

### Route B — Evidence on the Table

- Kitchen/workbench editorial still life: phone with abstract/non-readable app screen, handwritten symptom timeline, OBD scanner, repair receipt with fake/non-readable details, car key.
- Authentic wear and natural side light.
- Organized but not sterile.
- No fabricated customer outcome or readable private data.

### Route C — The Better Conversation

- Customer and independent mechanic beside a parked everyday vehicle in a real shop environment.
- Collaborative body language; neither person presented as a specific Drivable partner.
- Phone or printed symptom summary visible but not readable.
- No unsafe lift behavior, fake badges, exaggerated repairs, or implied endorsement.

## 10. Campaign Measurement

Track:

- Landing-page visits and application starts/completions.
- Qualified versus safety-ineligible applications.
- Audience/product-mode mix.
- Recruitment source.
- Acceptance and completion rate.
- Time/support burden per participant.
- Feedback usefulness and consent rates separately.
- Safety escalations and misleading-expectation feedback.

Do not optimize only for application volume. The campaign succeeds when it recruits appropriate participants who understand the boundaries and complete useful feedback.
