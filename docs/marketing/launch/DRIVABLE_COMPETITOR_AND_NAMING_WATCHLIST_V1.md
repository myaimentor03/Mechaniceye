# Drivable Competitor and Naming Watchlist V1

**Research date:** 2026-08-24  
**Evidence standard:** First-party product, support, terms, or store pages.  
**Purpose:** Repeatable launch-risk monitoring, not market-share analysis, trademark search, or legal opinion.

## Executive update

Current products already combine symptom-based AI guidance, ranked causes, urgency, repair costs, mechanic questions, buyer checks, saved vehicle histories, shareable diagnostic reports, seller workflows, or human handoffs. Drivable must not claim these features, individually or broadly combined, are first or unique.

The working launch position remains harder to prove: one permissioned evidence chain in which every material claim retains its source, uncertainty stays visible, and later findings do not rewrite earlier statements as facts.

## Factual naming signals

These are signals, not legal conclusions:

1. **Drivable** is used by an active automotive rental/listing marketplace, including **Drivable AI**.
2. **ClearSale** is used by an established Experian fraud-prevention business in transaction trust and human-plus-AI review.
3. **Buyer Check** appears as public product/report wording at AskCarl and CheckReg and descriptively in vehicle-inspection services.
4. **Mechanic’s Eye** appears as a descriptive automotive phrase in longstanding editorial use; PitStop AI currently uses “a mechanic’s eye” in marketing.
5. Public search findings do not establish registration, ownership, priority, geographic rights, confusion, infringement, or clearance.

## Naming decision requirements

Qualified counsel and the owner must separately assess Drivable, Drivable by Mechanic’s Eye, Mechanic’s Eye, Drivable Check, Buyer Check, Seller Check, ClearSale, Mechanic Match and Guided Service.

Review must cover intended jurisdictions, goods/services, company names, domains, app stores, social handles, trademark databases, common-law use, pronunciation and visual similarity, search confusion, coexistence constraints, filing strategy and accepted residual risk.

Until recorded:

- do not use **Drivable AI**;
- keep **ClearSale** provisional and out of irreversible public production;
- do not assume **Buyer Check** is exclusive or protectable;
- do not claim trademark clearance;
- keep paid media, filmed dialogue, licensed audio, physical materials and permanent branded exports reversible.

## Positioning consequences

### Supported direction

- “Designed so every material vehicle claim keeps its source.”
- “Possible causes remain possibilities until evidence or qualified human testing supports more.”
- “Buyer evidence, seller statements, observations and unknowns stay visibly distinct.”
- “A permissioned handoff can reduce repetition when human help is needed.”

### Prohibited direction

- first or only AI vehicle assistant;
- only app combining diagnosis and used-car checks;
- first shared vehicle record;
- nobody else connects owners, buyers, sellers and mechanics;
- safer or more accurate than competitors without comparative evidence;
- verified sellers, honest marketplace or vetted mechanics without functioning documented programs.

## Monitoring cadence

Monthly before launch, review each priority product’s homepage, product pages, store listings, help center, pricing, terms and major releases. Record only material changes affecting positioning, names, data sources, report persistence, human review, marketplaces, media analysis or mechanic fulfillment.

During the final six launch weeks, check weekly for exact-name search results, app-store titles, new feature names, overlapping campaign claims, changed terms and customer-confusion signals reaching Drivable support.

Immediately escalate a demand letter, platform complaint, takedown, app-store objection, domain dispute, impersonation, material confusion or newly discovered identical automotive product name. Do not respond publicly or admit a legal conclusion without owner-approved counsel.

## Evidence schema

Every future entry must record observation date, product, exact public claim, direct URL, page owner, affected Drivable claim or asset, factual interpretation, unresolved question, severity, reversible action and reviewer. If any item is unknown, retain the evidence and block the decision rather than inventing an answer.
