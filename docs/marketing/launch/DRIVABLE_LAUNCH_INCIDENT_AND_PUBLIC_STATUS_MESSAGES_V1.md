# Drivable Launch Incident and Public Status Messages V1

**Current operational state:** Not active; no public status channel or incident contact is approved.  
**Purpose:** Final, no-placeholder message library for use only when the stated incident is factually confirmed and the Communications Owner authorizes publication.

Do not publish every message for every issue. State only confirmed impact, avoid technical speculation, protect customer privacy, and update through the same official channel.

## 1. Severity Model

| Severity | Definition | Launch action |
|---|---|---|
| SEV-0 | Credible immediate physical-safety, widespread sensitive-data, or catastrophic integrity risk | Full pause, product containment, executive/legal/security coordination, direct affected-user action |
| SEV-1 | Major outage, wrong-user data exposure, widespread wrong reports, consent failure, or broken primary journey | Full traffic and campaign pause; rollback/degraded mode; public status update |
| SEV-2 | Material partial failure, severe latency, one workflow unavailable, support overload, or misleading creative | Pause affected wave/channel/workflow; status update when users are materially affected |
| SEV-3 | Minor degradation or isolated defect with safe workaround | Monitor, support directly, correct in normal release process |

## 2. Initial Investigation Message

**Title:** Drivable service issue under investigation

`We are investigating an issue affecting access to Drivable. We have paused launch promotion while we confirm the scope and protect the user experience. Drivable is not an emergency or vehicle-safety service. If a vehicle condition may be dangerous, stop using the vehicle and contact emergency services or a qualified professional as appropriate. We will update this notice when we have verified information.`

## 3. Service Waking or Severe Delay

**Title:** Drivable is taking longer than expected to load

`Drivable is currently taking longer than expected to become available for some visitors. We have paused additional launch traffic while the team restores reliable access. Do not repeatedly submit the same information. We will update this notice after performance is verified.`

## 4. Full Outage

**Title:** Drivable is temporarily unavailable

`Drivable is temporarily unavailable. New launch promotion is paused, and the team is working to restore the service safely. Do not use Drivable for an emergency or to determine whether a vehicle is safe to drive. We will post another update after service and data integrity are verified.`

## 5. Intake or Upload Failure

**Title:** Some Drivable submissions are not completing

`Some submissions are failing to complete. We have paused the affected intake and related promotion. Do not keep uploading the same files or assume an incomplete submission was received. We will restore the workflow only after submission status and evidence handling are verified.`

## 6. Report Delivery or Access Failure

**Title:** Some Drivable reports are not available

`Some users cannot access a report or submission status. We have paused the affected workflow while access and case integrity are checked. Do not rely on a missing or incomplete report as evidence that no vehicle issue exists. Drivable does not determine whether a vehicle is safe to drive.`

## 7. Incorrect or Cross-Vehicle Result

**Title:** Drivable result integrity issue

`We identified a result-integrity issue and have paused the affected service. Do not rely on a Drivable result received during the affected period. The team is isolating impacted records and will contact affected users through an authorized service channel when appropriate. Do not post vehicle evidence or report details publicly.`

## 8. Safety-Language or Unsafe-Output Incident

**Title:** Drivable safety review in progress

`We identified output that may not have communicated uncertainty or safety limits correctly. The affected experience and launch promotion are paused. Drivable does not provide a confirmed diagnosis or determine whether a vehicle is safe to drive. Anyone facing a potentially dangerous vehicle condition should stop using the vehicle and seek appropriate qualified or emergency help.`

## 9. Privacy or Wrong-Recipient Incident

**Title:** Drivable privacy investigation

`We are investigating a privacy issue and have paused the affected service and promotion. Do not forward, repost, or interact with vehicle evidence or a report that does not belong to you. We are preserving the facts, containing access, and will communicate directly with affected people as required. Public updates will avoid exposing personal information.`

## 10. Consent, Deletion, or Export Failure

**Title:** Drivable account-control issue

`We identified a problem affecting a privacy choice or data-control request. The affected workflow and related promotion are paused while the team verifies the request state and corrects the process. Do not resubmit a deletion, export, or consent request repeatedly; duplicate requests may make reconciliation harder.`

## 11. Mechanic's Eye Review Routing Defect

**Title:** Mechanic's Eye Review is unavailable

`The Mechanic's Eye Review link is not delivering the intended experience. It must not be used while the route and review scope are corrected. Drivable human review is not a physical inspection, confirmed diagnosis, emergency service, or vehicle-safety determination.`

## 12. Mechanic Match Capacity or Availability

**Title:** Mechanic Match requests are limited

`Mechanic Match request/referral support is currently limited. It is not live booking, and provider availability, specialty fit, credentials, price, timing, diagnosis, workmanship, and outcome are not guaranteed. We have paused promotion that could create additional requests until capacity is verified.`

## 13. Buyer Check or Seller-Provenance Defect

**Title:** Buyer and seller evidence labels under review

`We identified a problem affecting how a claim, observation, document, verification state, conflict, or unknown is displayed. The affected workflow is paused. A seller statement must not be treated as an observed or independently verified fact. Do not rely on the affected display while the provenance record is corrected.`

## 14. Misleading Advertisement or Creator Statement

**Title:** Correction to a Drivable promotional statement

`A Drivable promotional statement exceeded the product's verified capability. We removed or paused the material. The corrected position is: Drivable provides evidence-guided decision support; possible causes are not a confirmed diagnosis or vehicle-safety determination. We are reviewing where the earlier statement appeared and will correct the same audiences where practical.`

If the error concerns a different claim, publish the exact approved correction from the claim-to-proof matrix rather than improvising.

## 15. Naming or Affiliation Confusion

**Title:** Clarification about Drivable by Mechanic's Eye

`Drivable by Mechanic's Eye is not affiliated with the sports/luxury rental marketplace operating at drivable.app or with Experian's ClearSale fraud-prevention business. We are reviewing our naming and public materials and have paused affected promotion while that work continues.`

Use only if the statement is factually confirmed by the owner/legal record at publication time.

## 16. Recovery Message

**Title:** Drivable service restored

`The affected Drivable service has been restored after the team verified the correction and monitored the recovery. Launch promotion will resume only through the controlled traffic ramp. If you encountered the issue, review the current information at https://www.getdrivable.com/ rather than relying on an earlier incomplete page or result.`

Do not publish `resolved` until the responsible owner provides test evidence and monitoring confirms stability.

We will not send a concentrated wave of people into an experience that has not passed its safety, privacy, reliability, and claim-review gates. A new opening time will be announced only after those checks are complete.`

## 18. Launch Cancelled

**Title:** Drivable public opening cancelled

`The planned Drivable public opening has been cancelled. Existing product access and support status are described at https://www.getdrivable.com/. We will share future plans only after the underlying product, operational, and naming decisions are complete.`

## 19. Support Reply for Public Diagnosis Requests

`We cannot diagnose a personal vehicle or determine whether it is safe to drive in a public comment. Do not post private vehicle evidence here. Drivable is not an emergency service. If the condition may be dangerous, stop using the vehicle and contact emergency services or a qualified professional as appropriate.`

## 20. Support Reply for Seller-Fraud Allegations

`We cannot determine in a public comment whether a seller committed fraud. Preserve the listing, messages, documents, payment records, and relevant evidence without posting private information. Use appropriate platform, consumer-protection, law-enforcement, legal, or transaction-provider channels for your situation.`

## 21. Message Approval Checklist

- Incident and affected scope are confirmed.
- The message does not speculate about cause, blame, attackers, or recovery time.
- Safety and privacy boundaries are proportionate and clear.
- No personal, vehicle, report, security, or investigation detail is exposed.
- The same audiences that received a misleading statement receive the correction where practical.
- Channel, timestamp, version, approver, and next update are recorded in the incident log.
- Translations receive equivalent review.
- Recovery is verified before `resolved` is used.
