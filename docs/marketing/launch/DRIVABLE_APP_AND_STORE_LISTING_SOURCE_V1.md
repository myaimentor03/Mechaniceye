## 1. Distribution Decision Gate

Before using this copy, record:

- web app, progressive web app, iOS, Android, desktop, or other distribution path;
- supported devices, operating-system versions, languages, countries, and age requirements;
- account, subscription, payment, refund, and data-deletion behavior;
- diagnostic/medical-style, automotive, marketplace, user-generated-content, AI, privacy, safety, and location policy implications;
- actual release features versus disabled or roadmap features;
- review/test account and support owner;
- store developer-account ownership and two-factor authentication;
- privacy labels/data-safety form based on observed implementation, not marketing intent.

Do not open a developer account, pay fees, enroll in a platform program, accept legal agreements, or submit an app without founder authorization and company ownership controls.

## 5. Full Description — Drivable Check-Centered Release

### Start with what changed

Drivable by Mechanic's Eye helps drivers organize vehicle symptoms and available evidence before the next decision or professional conversation.

With Drivable Check, you can:

- describe what changed and when it happens;
- follow guided requests for relevant vehicle evidence;
- keep missing or unavailable evidence visible;
- review up to three confidence-ranked possible causes when the evidence supports them;
- see supporting, conflicting, and missing information;
- prepare clearer questions for a qualified professional;
- receive a request for more evidence or human review when uncertainty requires it.

### Evidence before certainty

Drivable is designed to avoid turning limited evidence into a confident-sounding answer. Sometimes the responsible result is that the available information is not sufficient to rank a possibility.

### Important limitations

Drivable provides informational decision support. It does not provide a confirmed diagnosis, repair instruction, inspection, valuation, title determination, emergency service, or determination that a vehicle is safe to drive. A qualified in-person professional may identify conditions not visible in submitted evidence.

Never handle a phone, type, photograph, record, or connect equipment while driving. Do not recreate a symptom or continue operating a vehicle merely to gather evidence. When a condition may be urgent or dangerous, stop using the vehicle and seek appropriate qualified or emergency help.

### Privacy and support

Only describe actual account, storage, sharing, correction, deletion, human-review, and support behavior in this section. Link the current privacy notice, terms, safety information, accessibility support, and contact route.

## 11. Review Notes and Customer Support

Prepare truthful reviewer instructions covering:

- test account and authentication;
- how to reach each submitted feature;
- which functionality requires human review or staged test data;
- permissions requested and why;
- safety-related workflow behavior;
- account/data deletion;
- subscription/payment behavior;
- contact information monitored during review.

Do not hide disabled behavior, remote content, payment conditions, or human operations from reviewers.

## 12. Ratings and Review Policy

- Ask for an honest review only at an appropriate, non-urgent moment.
- Do not condition access, support, compensation, incentives, or outcomes on a positive rating.
- Do not route dissatisfied users away from the platform review mechanism while prompting only satisfied users.
- Do not write, buy, exchange, fabricate, or have workers post reviews.
- Respond without exposing vehicle details, account status, or report findings.
- Treat safety, privacy, billing, discrimination, accessibility, and serious accuracy complaints as operational incidents, not reputation-management problems.
