# Drivable Testimonial and Media Permission Requirements V1

**Important:** This is a product/operations requirements document, not legal advice or a final release form. Qualified counsel should adapt the actual release to the company's entity, jurisdiction, participants, compensation, minors policy, and intended uses.

## 1. Permission Must Be Specific

A valid workflow should identify:

- Participant's legal identity in the restricted release record.
- Public identity choice: full name, first name/last initial, initials, pseudonym, anonymous, or no public identity.
- Exact quote/story/media approved.
- Whether Drivable may edit for length/clarity and how final approval works.
- Allowed channels: website, organic social, paid ads, email, press, app/store, presentation, partner material, or internal training.
- Allowed media: text, photo, video, audio, product screenshot, vehicle image, document excerpt, or other evidence.
- Paid advertising and creator/partner reuse status.
- Geography and duration.
- Compensation/material relationship and required disclosure.
- Revocation/takedown process and limitations for already distributed material.
- Contact for questions, correction, privacy, and withdrawal.

Blanket beta consent is not sufficient marketing permission.

## 2. Data Minimization

Default to excluding:

- Full VIN.
- License plate.
- Home/work address or precise location.
- Phone, email, account, case, payment, and title identifiers.
- Signatures and identity documents.
- Unrelated people or private messages.
- Shop/customer details not necessary to the story.
- Notification banners and background screen data.
- Document metadata and image geolocation where not needed.

Vehicle year/make/model, mileage, price, cost, and repair details can still identify a participant or transaction in context. Treat them deliberately.

## 3. People and Property

- Obtain permission from every identifiable person used in marketing.
- Establish a minors policy before accepting or filming anyone under legal adulthood.
- Obtain property/location permission where required.
- Do not assume a participant can authorize use of a mechanic, shop logo, private listing, seller messages, or third-party document.
- Remove or separately clear background faces, art, screens, music, logos, plates, uniforms, and signage.

## 4. Vehicle and Evidence Media

Permission to upload evidence for service does not automatically permit public marketing use.

For every selected item, record:

- Original evidence ID and owner/source.
- Service consent and marketing consent separately.
- What is visible/audible.
- Required redaction/crop/voice alteration.
- Whether the media could imply a diagnosis, inspection, safety status, ownership, or repair outcome.
- Approved caption and limitations.

## 5. Final Approval Workflow

1. Identify a story candidate without promising publication.
2. Confirm service feedback and outcome facts.
3. Obtain story-interview permission.
4. Draft the story with source/verification labels.
5. Complete internal claims, safety, privacy, and rights review.
6. Present exact identity, quote, media, channels, compensation disclosure, and duration for participant permission.
7. Store signed/recorded permission in a restricted company system.
8. Approve an exact asset version and publish only within scope.
9. Monitor context and comments for new harm or misunderstanding.
10. Re-review at expiry, material edit, new channel, or new paid use.

## 6. Participant Review Questions

Before approval, ensure the participant can answer yes:

- I understand participation is optional and not required for service/support.
- I have seen or understand the exact story, quote, and media being requested.
- I understand which name/identity level will appear.
- I understand where and for how long it may be used.
- I understand whether it may appear in paid advertising.
- I understand any compensation or material relationship and disclosure.
- I know how to request a correction or withdrawal.
- I am authorized to grant permission for the materials I provided.

## 7. Prohibited Practices

- Pre-checked marketing consent.
- Consent buried in general terms without a clear separate choice.
- Publishing before the intended person completes the final permission step.
- Using private evidence because it was already uploaded for service.
- Implying anonymous data cannot be re-identified without assessment.
- Using a negative experience as a positive claim through selective editing.
- Continuing use beyond agreed channel, geography, duration, or paid-use scope.
- Inventing a name, quote, customer, outcome, or photograph and presenting it as real.

## 8. Withdrawal and Correction

Maintain a documented workflow to:

- Authenticate the requester appropriately.
- Locate every active placement and derivative.
- Pause future scheduled use.
- Correct facts promptly.
- Remove owned-channel uses within the approved policy timeframe.
- Notify partners/platforms where required and track responses.
- Record archival/legal limitations without promising impossible deletion from all third-party copies.
- Prevent the retired asset from being re-uploaded.

## 9. Storage and Access

Release documents and identifiable proof belong in a restricted company-controlled system, not the public repository.

The repository may store:

- Blank requirements/templates.
- Non-sensitive story IDs.
- Approval status.
- Public final assets.
- Non-sensitive substantiation summaries.

It must not store signed releases, identity documents, private raw interviews, raw customer evidence, or contact information.
