# Drivable Coordinated Grand Opening Command System V1

**Launch model:** High-attention coordinated public opening  
**Schedule model:** Relative time from launch authorization; no calendar date is approved  
**Current state:** HOLD — P0 live-site, evidence/privacy, capacity, naming, and final-media gates are open  
**Official destination:** `https://www.getdrivable.com/`

This system is complete as an operating specification. It does not contain a hidden launch date, unnamed fill-in field, or authorization to publish.

## 1. Core Principle

A coordinated launch concentrates demand, scrutiny, and failure risk. It may begin only when the product can withstand synchronized press, creator, community, email, organic, and paid attention.

The launch sequence must amplify a verified experience. It must not use publicity to conceal missing evidence handling, privacy controls, service capacity, naming decisions, or product reliability.

## 2. Current Hold Reasons

The command clock cannot start while any of these remain open:

1. A fresh session can show a 30–40-second service-waking screen.
2. Mechanic's Eye Review routes to Disclaimer.
3. Photo, audio, video, OBD, and vibration claims exceed proven modality behavior.
4. No secure durable case/report-delivery contract is established.
5. Terms do not support a persistent evidence-chain promise.
6. Privacy and consent controls are not launch-complete.
7. Mobile navigation obscures the first-view product story.
8. Naming decisions for Drivable and ClearSale are unresolved.
9. No verified launch capacity, staffing, support, incident, payment, or campaign ownership exists.
10. Final rendered identity, product capture, testimonial proof, and launch assets are not approved.

## 3. Launch Authority Model

The following roles must be assigned to named, reachable humans in a restricted operations record before launch. An unassigned role is an automatic NO-GO.

| Role | Sole authority and responsibility |
|---|---|
| Launch Director | Starts, holds, resumes, or cancels the launch clock; owns final decision log |
| Product Release Owner | Confirms deployed behavior, known issues, release/rollback build, and product labels |
| Infrastructure Owner | Capacity, uptime, latency, errors, scaling, rollback, and status-page operations |
| Safety/Quality Owner | Safety language, high-risk outputs, human-review capacity, and unsafe-result escalation |
| Privacy/Security Owner | Consent, data flow, retention, deletion/export, providers, incidents, and breach coordination |
| Support Owner | Support coverage, scripts, queue capacity, response targets, and escalation |
| Marketing Operations Owner | Publishing queue, links, tracking, suppression, comments, and central campaign pause |
| Communications Owner | Press, creators, partners, public statements, corrections, and spokesperson control |
| Legal/Naming Owner | Records counsel/owner decisions and confirms permitted names/claims/geographies |
| Analytics Owner | Privacy-safe launch telemetry, data quality, dashboards, and anomaly reporting |

No agency, creator, platform representative, automation, or AI system receives final launch authority.

## 4. Single Control Channel

Before T-21 days, establish one access-controlled launch command channel and one durable decision log.

The decision log must record:

- timestamp and timezone;
- decision or observed incident;
- evidence link;
- affected product, route, audience, and channel;
- severity and customer impact;
- decision owner;
- action, deadline, and verification;
- whether campaigns were paused;
- public statement version if issued;
- resolution and retrospective owner.

Sensitive submissions, credentials, private reports, or customer evidence must not be pasted into the command channel.

## 5. Relative Launch Sequence

The clock begins only after all P0 gates close and the Launch Director records `CLOCK AUTHORIZED`.

### T-21 to T-15 — Proof Lock

- Freeze the public promise and released-product scope.
- Verify every claim against the claim-to-proof matrix.
- Freeze names subject to the recorded legal/owner decision.
- Complete the synthetic evidence-chain demonstration in the release build.
- Select only permissioned beta proof with substantiation records.
- Verify privacy, terms, consent, support, and deletion/export behavior against production.
- Complete load/cold-start, security, accessibility, device, and failure-state tests.
- Establish rollback build and maintenance/status experience.

**Exit:** No P0 defects; zero unresolved critical/high security or safety findings; all launch claims supported.

### T-14 to T-10 — Asset Lock

- Record final copy, media, caption, alt text, rights, source, approval, destination, and rollback asset.
- Export and visually inspect every channel crop.
- Capture the actual release build using synthetic demo data.
- Lock press fact sheet, FAQ, spokesperson brief, incident messages, and corrections process.
- Confirm creator/partner agreements and disclosures, if any.
- Keep scheduling disabled.

**Exit:** Every scheduled asset has a complete asset record; no placeholder or unavailable feature appears.

### T-9 to T-7 — Systems Rehearsal

- Run end-to-end form, consent, report, support, deletion/export, and error recovery tests.
- Rehearse launch traffic against the capacity model.
- Rehearse central campaign pause and content rollback.
- Rehearse P0 safety, privacy, outage, misleading-output, and naming complaints.
- Verify command-channel paging and role coverage.
- Test links from signed-out devices and external networks.

**Exit:** Rehearsal passes with timed evidence; failures are closed and re-tested.

### T-6 to T-4 — Controlled Teaser

Teaser may begin only after a separate teaser GO decision.

Allowed message:

`Evidence before certainty. Drivable is preparing a coordinated public opening. Availability and the official opening time will be announced only after final readiness review.`

Allowed content:

- evidence/provenance education;
- founder principles supported by real records;
- safe product details from the release build;
- launch-notification signup only if consent, preference, privacy, and support systems pass.

Not allowed:

- countdown timer without an approved opening time;
- unreleased feature reveal;
- accuracy, savings, user-count, network, or outcome claim without proof;
- ClearSale name amplification without clearance;
- promise that anyone who signs up will receive access.

### T-3 — Embargo Package

- Deliver approved materials only to signed/confirmed recipients.
- Embargo is a request/contract, not a technical guarantee.
- Include exact release scope, limits, official links, media rights, attribution, corrections contact, and opening time.
- Do not provide staged customer outcomes or unpublished metrics.
- Track receipt without adding recipients to unrelated marketing.

### T-2 — Final Technical Freeze

- No nonessential feature, dependency, analytics, copy, design, routing, or infrastructure changes.
- Only Launch Director-approved critical fixes may enter.
- Any critical fix resets the affected regression and rehearsal gates.

### T-1 — Final Go/No-Go

Every role gives one recorded status: GO, CONDITIONAL GO, or NO-GO.

`CONDITIONAL GO` must state a measurable condition resolved before opening. A verbal comfort statement is not a condition.

One NO-GO from Product, Infrastructure, Safety/Quality, Privacy/Security, Support, Legal/Naming, or Launch Director holds the launch.

### T-0 — Opening Ramp

Do not release all attention simultaneously. Use the ramp in Section 8.

### T+1 hour to T+24 hours — Stabilization

- Freeze nonessential changes.
- Monitor product, infrastructure, safety, privacy, support, conversion, and misinformation.
- Publish only approved corrections or status messages.
- Do not celebrate vanity metrics before data quality and customer impact are understood.

### T+2 to T+7 days — Evidence Review

- Compare demand, completion, support, error, safety, privacy, and outcome-quality signals.
- Separate traffic from successful evidence-chain completion.
- Retire misleading creative even if it converts.
- Expand only when capacity and quality stay inside gates.

## 6. Launch Go/No-Go Scorecard

Every row requires a linked evidence record. `Yes` without evidence is NO-GO.

| Gate | GO evidence | Automatic NO-GO |
|---|---|---|
| Naming | Written owner/counsel decision covers public names, geography, and uses | Drivable/ClearSale decision absent or contradicted |
| Availability | Public copy matches deployed release and route behavior | Roadmap marketed as live |
| Cold start | Fresh external sessions meet approved target with monitoring | Waking/loading interstitial or unexplained long delay |
| Capacity | Load test covers opening ramp with error/latency headroom | Untested synchronized demand |
| Routing | Every nav/CTA/deep link passes desktop/mobile/signed-out/reload | Review route or any primary CTA broken |
| Modalities | End-to-end byte/payload proof for every advertised input | Filename/metadata only, fake vibration, or unproven capable service |
| Evidence chain | Persistence, provenance, permissioned reuse, correction, deletion/export, and versioning pass | Terms or implementation deny the promise |
| Safety | Critical symptoms, insufficient evidence, and human review pass reviewed tests | Product implies safe-to-drive or hides uncertainty |
| Privacy | Actual data map, notice, consent, providers, retention, choices, contacts, and rights pass | Vague or implementation-mismatched notice |
| Security | No unresolved critical/high launch-scope findings; incident plan exercised | Known exploitable critical/high issue |
| Support | Staffed coverage and queue limits match ramp; scripts tested | No monitored route or demand overwhelms capacity |
| Accessibility | Keyboard, screen reader, focus, zoom, captions, contrast, errors, and mobile pass | Primary path inaccessible |
| Claims | Every exact claim supported; no prohibited claim | Unsupported accuracy, savings, trust, network, or superiority claim |
| Media | Rights, releases, provenance, alt text, captions, crops, and rollback complete | Unlicensed or fabricated proof |
| Analytics | Consent-aware events validated without sensitive evidence leakage | Broken measurement or sensitive payload in analytics |
| Pause/rollback | All channels and release can be paused/rolled back in rehearsal | No central stop authority |

## 7. Launch Metrics That Matter

Do not define success as impressions, clicks, signups, or press mentions alone.

### Product integrity

- successful start and completion rate;
- evidence request completion by modality;
- insufficient-evidence rate;
- human-review referral and capacity;
- report access/delivery success;
- correction, deletion/export, and support completion;
- unsafe or misleading output reports;
- provenance-label disputes.

### Reliability

- availability;
- cold-start incidence;
- p50/p95/p99 route and action latency;
- client/server error rate;
- upload/processing failure rate;
- queue age;
- rollback or degraded-mode events.

### Customer experience

- task completion;
- comprehension of possible cause versus diagnosis;
- comprehension of seller claim versus observation;
- accessibility failures;
- support demand and response time;
- opt-out, complaint, and abandonment patterns.

### Marketing quality

- qualified traffic by source;
- destination completion, not click alone;
- claim-related confusion by creative;
- organic saves/shares with negative-safety review;
- creator/press accuracy;
- cost only after data and consent integrity pass.

## 8. Traffic Ramp

The ramp protects quality without turning the launch into a concierge pilot.

### Wave 0 — Internal and instrumented verification

Duration: minimum 30 minutes immediately before public opening.  
Traffic: named team devices and external networks only.  
Required: all dashboards, support, and pause controls live.

### Wave 1 — Owned channels

Release homepage/product updates and one owned announcement. Hold email, creators, press embargo, communities, and paid media.

Observe for at least 30 minutes and until the minimum real-session sample established by the capacity test is reached.

Advance only if:

- no severity 0/1 event;
- error, latency, completion, support, and safety indicators remain inside tested bounds;
- public copy and routes behave as approved.

### Wave 2 — Press and committed creators

Lift embargo and activate coordinated editorial partners. Continue holding broad email and paid media.

Observe for at least 60 minutes and through the forecasted first attention peak.

### Wave 3 — Community and opted-in email

Release approved community posts and opted-in launch announcement. Apply frequency caps and support capacity limits.

### Wave 4 — Paid validation

Paid media begins only if organic/earned traffic proves message comprehension, product completion, consent, and support capacity. Start with the approved capped validation budget, not the mass-scale allocation.

### Wave 5 — Scale

Increase channel volume only in bounded increments after a complete review window. Any quality deterioration pauses the next increment.

## 9. Stop Conditions

Any authorized owner may call `HOLD`. The Launch Director may call `ABORT`. Safety/Quality and Privacy/Security owners may require immediate pause for their domains.

### Immediate full pause

- credible risk of physical harm caused or encouraged by output;
- safe-to-drive implication from an unverified result;
- wrong-user evidence/report exposure;
- credential, payment, or sensitive-data exposure;
- material consent or deletion failure;
- widespread wrong, fabricated, or cross-vehicle report content;
- site unavailable or stuck waking for a material share of launch traffic;
- broken primary intake/report route;
- public naming/legal instruction to stop;
- inability to pause campaigns or support affected users.

### Pause affected channel/creative

- misleading headline or missing material qualifier;
- wrong destination or tracking;
- creator/press statement exceeds approved facts;
- inaccessible creative or landing experience;
- comment volume exceeds safe moderation capacity;
- abnormal complaints, opt-outs, or low-quality traffic;
- platform policy warning.

### Hold next traffic wave

- latency, errors, queues, support, or human review approach tested limits;
- evidence completion falls below the rehearsal range;
- users consistently misunderstand diagnosis, safety, or provenance;
- analytics quality is insufficient to judge safety and completion.

## 10. Decision Rules

- Revenue, conversion, press, or sunk media cost never overrides safety, privacy, security, or truthful claims.
- Do not wait for statistical certainty when credible harm is possible.
- Preserve evidence before editing/deleting a public asset, subject to privacy/security rules.
- Correct the public record where the original message reached users; a silent edit may be insufficient.
- Resume only after the responsible owner verifies closure and the Launch Director records the decision.

## 11. Completion Criteria

This command system is ready to operate when:

- every role is assigned in a restricted record;
- every P0 gate has linked closure evidence;
- a launch date and timezone are approved in the restricted operations calendar;
- the complete publishing queue contains no unresolved values;
- incident, pause, rollback, and public-message rehearsals pass;
- the Launch Director records `CLOCK AUTHORIZED`.

Until then, current state remains `HOLD`.
