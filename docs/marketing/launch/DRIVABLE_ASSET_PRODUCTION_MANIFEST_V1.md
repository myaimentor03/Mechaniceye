## 9. Proof-Dependent Asset Rules

The following statements remain blocked until a substantiation record identifies the sample, method, period, exclusions, reviewer, and approved wording:

- Diagnostic or cause-identification accuracy.
- Percent of users helped.
- Money or time saved.
- Repairs avoided.
- Vehicles safely kept on the road.
- Sales completed or price improvements.
- Size, quality, or availability of the mechanic network.
- Customer counts, star ratings, or satisfaction percentages.
- “Best,” “most trusted,” “leading,” or comparative superiority claims.

Customer quotes require permission and must not be edited into a broader claim than the customer made.

## 10. File Naming and Source Requirements

Use:

`DRV_<asset-id>_<campaign>_<concept>_<ratio-or-size>_<locale>_vNN_<status>.<ext>`

Example:

`DRV_SO-002_evidence-first_dashboard-photo_4x5_en-US_v01_review.png`

Each final asset must retain:

- Editable master where applicable.
- Export file.
- Copy/caption source.
- Rights/release/license record.
- Approval state and approver.
- Production date and version.
- Campaign, channel, audience, CTA, landing page, and UTM mapping.
- For generated imagery: tool/model path, final prompt, creation date, and human QA result.
