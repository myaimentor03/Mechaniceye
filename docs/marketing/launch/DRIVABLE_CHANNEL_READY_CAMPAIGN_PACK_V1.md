## Comment Response Bank

**“Can you tell me exactly what is wrong?”**  
Drivable Check is designed to rank possible causes from available evidence, not guarantee an exact diagnosis. Some cases need more evidence, human review, or an in-person professional.

The current promoted release does not claim recording upload or analysis. Use only the input methods shown in the released workflow, and never record while driving.

# Campaign C — Better Mechanic Questions

**Master:** `assets/social/DRV_SO-003_mechanic-conversation_ask-for-evidence_4x5_v01_review.svg`  
**Objective:** Consumer education without attacking mechanics  
**Audience:** Vehicle owners reviewing an estimate  
**Destination after launch gates close:** `https://www.getdrivable.com/`  
**UTM concept:** `ask-for-evidence`

## Organic Caption

A better repair conversation does not begin with an accusation. It begins with clear questions:

- What evidence supports this recommended work?
- Which work is urgent, preventive, or able to wait—and why?
- What tests, parts, labor, fees, tax, and warranty are included?
- Will you contact me before the authorized amount changes?

Good questions can support good professionals and help customers understand the decision in front of them.

No phrase by itself proves that a mechanic is dishonest.

## Short-Form Video Voiceover — 20 seconds

Before approving a repair, ask what evidence supports the work. Ask what is urgent and why. Confirm what the estimate includes, what the warranty covers, and whether the shop will call before the total changes. Clear questions support a better conversation. No phrase alone proves that a mechanic is dishonest.

## On-Screen Text

1. `What evidence supports the work?`

3. `What does the estimate include?`
4. `Call me before the total changes.`
5. `Good questions support good professionals.`

# Campaign D — Unknown Is a Responsible Result

**Master:** `assets/social/DRV_SO-004_confidence_unknown-is-a-result_4x5_v01_review.svg`  
**Objective:** Explain bounded confidence and reduce demand for false certainty  
**Audience:** Beta prospects, customers, partners, journalists  
**Destination after launch gates close:** `https://www.getdrivable.com/`  
**UTM concept:** `unknown-is-a-result`

## Organic Caption

An AI product should not sound more certain than its evidence.

In Drivable, confidence is intended to show:

- what supports a possibility;
- what conflicts with it;
- what is missing;
- what could change the ranking;
- when human or in-person review is needed.

Sometimes the responsible result is: unable to rank from the evidence available.

Confidence is not a safety rating. Possible causes are not a confirmed diagnosis.

## Founder Post Variant

One of the hardest product decisions is allowing the system to say “the current evidence is not enough.” That answer may look less impressive in a demo, but it is essential to building Drivable responsibly. We are designing confidence to expose support, conflict, and missing evidence—not to decorate a guess with a percentage.

## Alt Text

Dark navy chart showing higher, moderate, low, and unable-to-rank confidence states. The final state explains that evidence may be too limited, unsafe to collect, out of scope, or conflicting.

## Press Talking Point

Drivable treats an inability to rank responsibly as a valid product outcome rather than forcing a diagnostic-sounding answer from weak evidence.

# Campaign E — ClearSale: Claims Are Not Facts

**Master:** `assets/social/DRV_SO-010_clearsale_claims-are-not-facts_4x5_v01_review.svg`

**Audience:** Used-car buyers, responsible sellers, industry observers  
**Destination after naming and launch gates close:** `https://www.getdrivable.com/`  
**UTM concept:** `claims-are-not-facts`

## Organic Caption

If a seller says “no accidents,” AI can rewrite that sentence—but rewriting does not verify it.

ClearSale is being designed so the source stays visible:

- Seller stated
- Observed in submitted evidence
- Document supplied
- Independently verified
- Unknown
- Conflicting information

The goal is not to promise that every person will be honest. It is to make claims, evidence, verification, and unknowns harder to blur together.

ClearSale is coming soon. The example shown is synthetic educational content, not a real listing or customer result.

## Short Post

AI can draft a listing. It cannot promote a seller statement into an observed fact. ClearSale is a provisional product name for a planned workflow; naming clearance is pending. Official Drivable information is at `https://www.getdrivable.com/`.

## Alt Text

Light ClearSale graphic tracing “Seller stated: no accidents” through an AI-generated listing draft to a Buyer Check label that says the statement remains a seller claim and verification is unavailable.

## Comment Response Bank

**“So every ClearSale listing is verified?”**  
No. Each field is intended to show whether it is seller-stated, observed in submitted evidence, supported by a supplied document, independently verified, unknown, or conflicting.

**“Does this stop fraud?”**  
No system can guarantee that. ClearSale's design goal is to preserve provenance and expose unknowns so misleading information is harder to present without context.

**“Can I list a car now?”**  
Not yet. Seller Check is a planned pilot workflow, and the ClearSale marketplace is coming soon.

# 4. Accessibility and Localization Controls

- Alt text describes the information conveyed, not every decorative shape.
- Burned-in text must also appear in the post copy or accessible page.
- Videos require accurate captions, readable on-screen text, a transcript, and no information communicated only through sound or color.
- Do not use all-caps body paragraphs or rapid flashing/motion.
- Localized copy must be reviewed by a fluent human for automotive meaning, safety language, reading level, and regional terminology.
- Do not translate product names unless an approved naming decision exists.
- Re-check line lengths and visual hierarchy after translation.

# 5. Comment and Crisis Triage

Hide or remove content only under a written moderation policy. Preserve reports involving safety, privacy, threats, fraud, impersonation, or evidence misuse for the designated reviewer.

| Comment type | Response |
|---|---|
| General product question | Answer using current release label and approved limits |
| Request for personal diagnosis in comments | Do not diagnose; route to approved intake or qualified help |
| Possible immediate safety issue | Use conservative stop/escalate language; do not wait for marketing approval |
| Complaint about result accuracy | Move to private support; preserve submission reference and escalate |
| Privacy or evidence complaint | Stop ordinary engagement and route to privacy/incident owner |
| Mechanic accusation | Do not validate dishonesty from a phrase or anecdote; encourage documentation and appropriate consumer channels |
| ClearSale fraud allegation | Preserve evidence, avoid public adjudication, route to marketplace/incident owner when that program exists |
| Press inquiry | Route to approved spokesperson and press kit |

# 6. Paid Activation Gate

No paid campaign may move from `disabled template` to `channel_ready` until all are recorded:

- verified product/release scope and matching landing page;
- tested conversion and consent flow;
- privacy and legal review for channel, audience, targeting, and geography;
- claim review and substantiation record where applicable;
- support owner and capacity limit;
- approved budget, daily cap, total cap, stop-loss condition, and payment owner;
- account ownership, least-privilege access, and two-factor authentication;
- UTM and privacy-safe analytics QA;
- creative visual, accessibility, crop, caption, and destination review;
- comment moderation and incident escalation coverage;
- documented pause authority.

Paid media should begin with a bounded learning test after organic/message validation—not a mass-launch spend intended to compensate for missing evidence.
