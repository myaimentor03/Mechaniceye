# Drivable Brand Usage One-Sheet — Source V1

**Status:** Provisional beta production source. Rendered one-sheet/PDF, trademark screening, and founder approval remain pending.

## Brand

**Drivable by Mechanic's Eye** helps bring order to uncertain vehicle decisions through evidence, visible limits, and better next questions.

**Brand principle:** Evidence before certainty.

## Name

- Master brand: **Drivable by Mechanic's Eye**
- Consumer short form: **Drivable**
- Product family: **Drivable Check**, **Buyer Check**, **Seller Check**, **ClearSale**, **Mechanic Match**, **Guided Service**
- The apostrophe in **Mechanic's Eye** is required.
- Do not use `AI Mechanic`, `Drivable Certified`, `Mechanic's Eye Approved`, or diagnostic/safety language as a brand name.

## Mark

Use only the current reviewed master appropriate to the background:

- dark lockup on Midnight surfaces;
- light lockup on Paper/light surfaces;
- one-color lockup when color reproduction is unavailable;
- provisional app icon only after small-size and mask review.

Keep clear space around the lockup equal to at least the height of the lowercase `d` bowl in the wordmark. Do not place copy, page edges, faces, vehicle features, or another logo inside that space.

Minimum working sizes before rendered review:

- horizontal digital lockup: 160 px wide;
- endorsement lockup in print: 35 mm wide;
- app/social icon: follow platform export requirements and verify at 16 px through 512 px.

These are working thresholds, not final production specifications.

### Never

- stretch, skew, rotate, outline, bevel, shadow, glow, or recolor the mark;
- detach or rearrange symbol elements;
- put the mark over a busy or low-contrast image;
- use the app symbol as a checkmark, safety badge, certification, or confidence score;
- add an eye, wrench, gear, car silhouette, robot, shield, or check-engine icon;
- redraw from a screenshot or AI-generated approximation;
- imply approval by placing the mark beside an unverified seller, provider, vehicle, outcome, or claim.

## Type

Working digital stack: `Inter, Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif`.

- Use sentence case for body copy and most headings.
- Reserve all caps for short eyebrows or status labels.
- Keep body copy plain, direct, and readable.
- Do not use condensed racing fonts, futuristic “AI” display type, distressed shop type, or handwritten scripts.
- Keep editable text masters. Outline type only in locked final distribution files.

Font licensing and final production families must be confirmed before public launch.

## Voice

**Calm, evidence-first, respectful, and bounded.**

Say:

- `up to three confidence-ranked possible causes`
- `based on the evidence provided`
- `supported positives, risks, and unknowns`
- `seller stated`
- `observed in submitted evidence`
- `human review recommended`

Do not say:

- `we diagnosed it`
- `know exactly what is wrong`
- `safe to drive`
- `never get ripped off again`
- `honest marketplace guaranteed`
- `vetted mechanic` before the vetting program exists

## Photography

- Use real, ordinary vehicles and people; calm documentary framing; natural light; credible environments.
- Show phone and evidence collection only while safely parked.
- Include a range of ages, bodies, abilities, vehicles, neighborhoods, and economic contexts without stereotypes.
- Keep dashboards, plates, documents, addresses, shop signs, and bystanders private unless permissioned.
- Avoid cinematic breakdown panic, luxury-only imagery, aggressive driving, staged distress, fake mechanics, unsafe inspection, and diagnostic certainty.
- Generated imagery must retain prompt/source records and human QA; never present it as a customer story or product outcome.

## Product Imagery

- Use synthetic demo data that cannot be mistaken for a real person, vehicle, report, seller, or outcome.
- Label mockups and staged scenarios.
- Do not fabricate testimonials, metrics, results, map coverage, marketplace inventory, providers, ratings, or waitlist numbers.
- Preserve seller-claim provenance in every listing example.
- Keep missing evidence and uncertainty visible.

## Mandatory Boundaries

- Possible causes are not a confirmed diagnosis.
- Drivable does not determine whether a vehicle is safe to drive.
- Never collect evidence while driving.
- Seller statements remain claims unless independently observed or verified.
- ClearSale cannot guarantee honesty, completeness, condition, title, or transaction outcome.
- Mechanic Match cannot promise provider availability, quality, price, or outcome.

## File and Approval

Use the canonical asset ID, version, status, editable source, export, copy, rights record, approval record, and destination mapping. `review` and `drafted` assets must not be placed into public channel folders or represented as approved.

Before public use, confirm:

- rendered visual QA;
- accessibility and contrast;
- trademark/name screening;
- product and claim review;
- permissions and licenses;
- current release status and CTA;
- channel crop and file requirements;
- approver, date, expiration, and rollback asset.
