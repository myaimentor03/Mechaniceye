## Complete organic captions

### Card 1

Before pursuing a used vehicle, look for what is supported, what conflicts, and what is missing. Keep seller statements separate from observed or supplied evidence so a confident summary does not erase its source.

Buyer Check does not verify the seller, title, mileage, current condition, or transaction and is not a pre-purchase inspection.

### Card 2

A supplied record may support part of a seller's statement without proving the vehicle's present condition or that earlier work resolved the issue.

The source, date, scope, and remaining unknowns should stay visible. The example shown is educational and synthetic—not a real listing or result.

Buyer Check does not independently verify the seller or supplied records.

### Card 3

When two sources appear to conflict, preserve both. Record who said what, when each record was created, and what each source actually establishes. Then ask a focused follow-up question.

A conflict is useful information, but it is not automatic proof of deception. This example is synthetic education and says nothing about a real seller or vehicle.

### Card 4

Buyer Check can organize seller statements, supplied records, supported positives, risks, conflicts, missing information, and next questions. A qualified independent inspection can examine the current vehicle within an agreed scope. The buyer makes the decision using the sources appropriate to the transaction.

Buyer Check is not an inspection and does not verify title, mileage, current condition, seller identity, or transaction terms.

## Seller-claim and safety review

- Seller statements remain explicitly labeled `SELLER-REPORTED` or `SELLER STATEMENT`.
- A supplied service record supports only what that record states; it does not independently verify the seller, current condition, repair effectiveness, or completeness.
- The synthetic conflict card says a conflict is not automatic proof of deception.
- No AI-written sentence turns a seller claim into an observed fact.
- No vehicle is described as problem-free, fixed, verified, inspected, mechanically sound, safe, roadworthy, certified, or guaranteed.
- The set does not imply title, mileage, identity, history, condition, or transaction verification.
- It clearly distinguishes Buyer Check from an independent pre-purchase inspection.
