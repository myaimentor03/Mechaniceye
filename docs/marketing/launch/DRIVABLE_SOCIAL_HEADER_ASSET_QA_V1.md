## Design and claim review

- The three files are separately composed for their intended canvas rather than automatic crops of one master.
- The visual system uses the provisional Drivable mark, dark navy field, evidence-path motif, cyan active point, amber unresolved point, and neutral support text.
- “Evidence before certainty” describes the intended decision posture. It does not promise diagnostic accuracy, safety, inspection, repair results, verified sellers, mechanic quality, or marketplace outcomes.
- “Organize what you know. Keep unknowns visible.” preserves uncertainty and does not imply that seller-reported information has been independently verified.
- No product availability, beta enrollment, date, price, statistic, customer quote, rating, or contact detail is invented.
- Each SVG has a descriptive title and description and uses editable native shapes and text without external image dependencies.
- PNG exports were rendered locally and visually inspected for clipping, font fallback, contrast, line continuity, and balance.

## Platform caveat

Platform header crops and profile-image overlays change across device, account type, and interface revisions. These review assets use conservative central composition, but actual publishing-account previews are required before approval. If a platform preview obscures identity or text, create a new versioned master; do not overwrite these review files.

## Release gates

- owner approval of the visual direction;
- qualified naming/trademark decision for Drivable;
- confirmation of the exact account type and current platform specifications;
- in-account desktop and mobile crop preview;
- accessible profile description and destination link review;
- confirmed account ownership, recovery, and publishing authority;
- final export naming, checksum, approver, and date recorded in the manifest.
