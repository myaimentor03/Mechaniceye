# Drivable Channel Publishing and Measurement Runbook V1

**Status:** Pre-beta operating template  
**Brand:** Drivable by Mechanic's Eye  
**Current campaign authorization:** Closed-beta recruitment only after the application flow and operational gate pass.

## 1. Publication Principle

An approved creative file is not automatically ready to publish. A placement is channel-ready only when the exact asset, caption, destination, tracking, consent, audience, dates, owner, and stop conditions are attached and verified.

## 2. Campaign Naming

Use:

`drv_<stage>_<product>_<objective>_<audience>_<geo>_<start-yyyymmdd>`

Examples:

- `drv_beta_drivable-check_recruit_owner-local_wa_20260915`
- `drv_beta_buyer-check_recruit_private-buyer_wa_20260915`

Ad/content identifier:

`<asset-id>_<concept>_<format>_<version>`

Example:

`WB-003_due-diligence_4x5_v01`

## 3. UTM Convention

| Parameter | Rule | Example |
|---|---|---|
| `utm_source` | Lowercase platform or partner. | `instagram`, `facebook`, `youtube`, `email`, `community-partner` |
| `utm_medium` | Controlled medium. | `organic-social`, `paid-social`, `cpc`, `email`, `referral`, `pr` |
| `utm_campaign` | Exact campaign name. | `drv_beta_buyer-check_recruit_private-buyer_wa_20260915` |
| `utm_content` | Exact creative/ad identifier. | `WB-003_due-diligence_4x5_v01` |
| `utm_term` | Search keyword or documented audience variant only. | `used-car-inspection-questions` |

Rules:

- Lowercase ASCII and hyphens only; no spaces or personal information.
- Never place a VIN, case ID, customer email, mechanic name, or private group/member identifier in a URL.
- Do not shorten a link until the final destination and tracking parameters are verified.
- Preserve a mapping from shortened link to full URL and owner.

## 4. Channel-Ready Record

Every placement requires:

- Campaign and placement IDs.
- Product and readiness label.
- Audience and excluded audiences.
- Channel/account and account owner.
- Exact asset filename/version.
- Exact headline, body, caption, CTA, alt text, and boundary language.
- Destination and complete UTM URL.
- Start/end time and timezone.
- Organic/paid/partner status.
- Budget, bid/optimization, and stop-loss if paid.
- Product, safety/claims, rights/privacy, and final channel approvals.
- Monitoring owner and escalation contact.
- Screenshot or archive of the live placement.

## 5. Pre-Publish Checklist

### Product and Offer

- [ ] Product readiness matrix permits this availability label and CTA.
- [ ] Destination flow works from a private/incognito browser on mobile and desktop.
- [ ] Form submission, confirmation, error handling, notifications, and operator intake are tested.
- [ ] Availability, geography, eligibility, turnaround, and capacity limits are visible.
- [ ] The advertised feature and product route use consistent naming.

### Creative

- [ ] Exact version is approved under the claims/creative review policy.
- [ ] Headline and first frame match the landing-page promise.
- [ ] No mock interface or generated person is presented as a real outcome/customer.
- [ ] Required boundary is readable and not hidden behind “more.”
- [ ] Crop, safe areas, captions, audio level, spelling, contrast, and alt text pass review.
- [ ] Rights, releases, licenses, and generated-asset provenance are attached.

### Tracking and Privacy

- [ ] UTM parameters match this runbook.
- [ ] Analytics and conversion events fire once and contain no sensitive intake data.
- [ ] Consent/privacy language matches actual tracking and follow-up behavior.
- [ ] Retargeting or custom audiences are explicitly approved and configured within platform policy.
- [ ] Test traffic can be excluded from reporting.

### Operations

- [ ] Named person/task owns monitoring and responses.
- [ ] Human-review/support capacity is sufficient for the planned reach.
- [ ] Frequently asked questions and escalation answers are ready.
- [ ] Safety-critical messages are not handled as ordinary social support.
- [ ] Correction, takedown, pause, refund, complaint, and incident paths are documented.

## 6. Closed-Beta Channel Kit

### Authorized Objective

Recruit appropriate adults with non-emergency Drivable Check or Buyer Check cases who understand that the product is in closed beta.

### Recommended Initial Channels

1. Founder personal outreach.
2. Permission-based local/community posts.
3. Existing email contacts with a legitimate relationship and appropriate consent.
4. Small organic social posts.
5. Independent-shop/professional feedback outreach.

Do not start paid acquisition until the controlled channels prove application quality, service capacity, and message comprehension.

### Standard Readiness Labels

- Drivable Check — Closed beta in preparation.
- Buyer Check — Closed beta in preparation.
- Mechanic Match — Manual referral pilot planned.
- Seller Check — Pilot workflow planned.
- ClearSale Marketplace — Coming soon.
- Guided Service — Future roadmap.

## 7. Measurement Framework

### Funnel

- Qualified reach/visits.
- Application start and completion.
- Safety-ineligible or out-of-scope application rate.
- Accepted participant rate.
- Intake completion.
- Report/review completion.
- Feedback completion.
- Optional verified outcome completion.

### Quality and Safety

- Percent of applicants who correctly understand “not a diagnosis/safety clearance.”
- Incorrect expectation reports.
- Critical-risk escalations.
- Reports held/requesting evidence/do-not-send.
- Corrections, complaints, privacy requests, and adverse events.
- Reviewer/support time and queue age.

### Creative Learning

- Hook and concept.
- Product/audience pairing.
- Landing-page continuation.
- Qualified application rate—not just click-through rate.
- Completion and expectation quality by source.
- Comment/question themes and confusion.

Do not optimize toward clicks or application volume if safety-ineligible, poor-fit, or misunderstood applications rise.

## 8. Paid Test Gate and Stop-Loss

Before the first paid test, define:

- Maximum total and daily budget.
- Maximum acceptable cost per qualified completed application.
- Minimum sample before a creative decision, where practical.
- Maximum support/review queue age.
- Maximum misleading-expectation and safety-ineligible rates.
- Named person authorized to pause spend.

Pause immediately when:

- The destination or form fails.
- A claim or availability statement is inaccurate.
- Review/support capacity exceeds its limit.
- A privacy, security, serious complaint, or adverse-event concern emerges.
- Tracking is duplicating or leaking sensitive data.
- Platform comments/messages reveal widespread dangerous misunderstanding.

## 9. Launch-Day Monitoring

At public launch, use a shared command log with:

- Timestamp and timezone.
- Deployment/product health.
- Traffic and conversion quality.
- Review/support queue.
- Payment/refund state if applicable.
- Safety, complaint, privacy, fraud, and abuse incidents.
- Channel status and spend.
- Decision, owner, and next check time.

Do not expand spend during an unresolved product, safety, privacy, or fulfillment incident.

## 10. Post-Publication Review

Within the defined review window:

1. Capture the live placement and exact destination.
2. Confirm tracking and conversion quality.
3. Review public comments/messages for misunderstanding and harm signals.
4. Compare qualified behavior across creative—not only platform-reported clicks.
5. Mark the asset `retain`, `iterate`, `pause`, or `retire` with a reason.
6. Record what changed and whether re-approval is required.

No performance result authorizes a broader marketing claim unless the substantiation process separately approves the exact claim.
