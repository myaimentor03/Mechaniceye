# Drivable Product Landing Page Kits V1

Page-ready information architecture and copy for **Drivable by Mechanic's Eye**. These kits are source material, not authority to publish. Calls to action and availability labels must be checked against the repository readiness matrix immediately before release.

## Shared Page Requirements

Every product page must include:

- the current availability label near the first CTA;
- a plain-language statement of what the product does and does not do;
- an explanation of evidence, confidence, missing information, and human review where relevant;
- capture safety before any evidence-collection instruction;
- privacy, consent, terms, support, and accessibility links before submission;
- no mock interface presented as a real customer result;
- no seller statement presented as an observation or verified fact;
- no unsupported performance, savings, trust, safety, network, or outcome claim.

Recommended global navigation:

`Drivable Check | Buyer Check | ClearSale | Mechanic Match | How evidence works | Safety & limits`

Until expansion products are available, label their navigation destinations `Coming soon` or `Pilot planned` rather than presenting inactive controls.

# A. Drivable Check Page Kit

**Asset:** WB-002  
**Current label:** Closed beta in preparation  
**Primary audience:** Driver who noticed a symptom or warning and wants a clearer next step  
**Primary CTA:** Join the beta waitlist  
**Secondary CTA:** See how evidence works

## Hero

**Eyebrow:** Drivable Check · Closed beta in preparation

**Headline:** Start with what changed.

**Subhead:** Drivable Check guides you through useful vehicle evidence and organizes up to three confidence-ranked possible causes—so you can approach the next conversation with better questions, not invented certainty.

**Primary CTA:** Join the beta waitlist

**Secondary CTA:** See how the check works

**Hero qualifier:** Possible causes are not a confirmed diagnosis, repair instruction, or determination that a vehicle is safe to drive.

**Art direction:** A real parked driver reviewing a calm evidence checklist, with the vehicle stationary and phone use clearly safe. Do not show a result screen containing fabricated findings.

## Problem Section

**Heading:** A new sound should not begin with a blind guess.

When a warning light appears or a vehicle feels different, it can be hard to explain what happened. Important details are often scattered across memory, dashboard messages, recent work, and repair history. Drivable Check is designed to organize those written observations into a clearer starting point.

## How It Works

### 1. Describe the change

Record when the symptom began, when it appears, and what changed recently. You do not need to name the failed part.

### 2. Add the information the current release supports

The current release may request written symptom details, timing, dashboard information, recent work, and user-entered OBD information. Every request should explain why the information matters and allow `I can't provide this`. Do not advertise file, photo, audio, video, document, or vibration processing until a release-level test proves the relevant bytes reach a capable service and affect a sourced result.

### 3. Review bounded possibilities

When the evidence supports it, the report may present up to three possible causes, confidence, supporting evidence, conflicting information, and missing evidence.

### 4. Decide the next conversation

Use the report to prepare questions for a qualified professional. Safety or uncertainty may require human review or an in-person inspection.

## Confidence Explainer

**Heading:** Confidence should show its work.

Drivable should not turn a weak signal into a strong conclusion. A result must make clear:

- what evidence supports the possibility;
- what evidence conflicts with it;
- what remains unknown;
- what additional evidence could change the ranking;
- whether human or in-person review is recommended.

Low confidence is not a failed experience. Sometimes the honest answer is that the current evidence is not enough.

## Safety Panel

**Heading:** Park first. Evidence second.

Never handle a phone, type, photograph, record, or connect equipment while driving. Do not approach moving, hot, energized, pressurized, or unsupported components. If a symptom may indicate immediate danger, stop using the vehicle and contact emergency services or a qualified professional as appropriate.

## Beta CTA

**Heading:** Help build a more evidence-first vehicle conversation.

Join the waitlist for a limited beta designed to test instructions, evidence quality, confidence communication, and support—not to manufacture promotional success stories.

**CTA:** Join the beta waitlist

**Below CTA:** Joining does not guarantee selection, access date, or continued availability.

## FAQ

**Does Drivable Check diagnose my car?**  
No. It may identify up to three confidence-ranked possible causes from the evidence available. A qualified in-person diagnosis may reach a different conclusion.

**Does it tell me whether I can keep driving?**  
No. It does not determine roadworthiness or safety. Urgent symptoms require conservative action and qualified help.

**What if I do not have OBD data or another requested detail?**  
Say that you cannot provide it. The report should preserve the missing evidence as uncertainty rather than inviting a guess.

**Will every submission receive an automated answer?**  
No. The beta may request more evidence, require human review, or be unable to support a useful result.

**Is my feedback used in advertising?**  
Not automatically. Marketing use of your name, likeness, vehicle, evidence, quote, or outcome requires separate permission.

# B. Buyer Check Page Kit

**Asset:** WB-003  
**Current label:** Closed beta in preparation  
**Primary audience:** Used-car shopper deciding what to inspect, verify, or ask next  
**Primary CTA:** Join the Buyer Check beta list  
**Secondary CTA:** See a sample evidence map

## Hero

**Eyebrow:** Buyer Check · Closed beta in preparation

**Headline:** Check what is supported. Notice what is missing.

**Subhead:** Buyer Check helps organize listing and vehicle evidence into supported positives, risks, seller claims, and unknowns before you make a decision.

**Primary CTA:** Join the Buyer Check beta list

**Secondary CTA:** Learn how claims are labeled

**Hero qualifier:** Buyer Check is not a complete inspection, title guarantee, valuation, safety determination, or promise of future reliability.

## Core Distinction

**Heading:** “Seller stated” is not the same as “observed.”

Buyer Check is designed to preserve where each statement came from:

| Label | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Seller stated | The seller supplied the statement; Drivable has not converted it into fact |
| Observed in submitted evidence | The visible or recorded evidence supports the limited observation shown |
| Document supplied | A document was provided but may not have been independently authenticated |
| Independently verified | A recorded verification method supports the exact field |
| Unknown / not provided | The information is missing or unavailable |
| Conflicting information | Available sources do not agree |

## What the Shopper Receives

- supported positive observations without implying the entire vehicle is “good”;
- material risks visible in available evidence;
- seller claims kept in their own category;
- missing or conflicting information that deserves a question;
- a focused list of items for independent verification or inspection.

## Scenario Section

**Heading:** A clean-looking listing can still contain unknowns.

Example demo scenario:

- Seller stated: `No accidents.`
- Submitted information: no independent evidence about structural history.
- Document status: no independent history source connected.
- Buyer Check output: `Seller claim; verification unavailable. Request history documentation and an independent inspection.`

Label this scenario as synthetic demonstration data. Do not imply it is a real listing or customer outcome.

## Independent Verification Panel

Buyer Check should complement—not discourage—an in-person pre-purchase inspection, title and ownership review, recall research, test drive performed safely and lawfully, financing review, and independent assessment appropriate to the transaction.

## CTA

**Heading:** Bring better questions to the next used-car decision.

**CTA:** Join the Buyer Check beta list

**Below CTA:** Beta eligibility, coverage, response time, and available evidence sources may vary.

## FAQ

**Does Buyer Check certify the vehicle?**  
No. It organizes available evidence and unknowns; it does not certify condition, title, value, safety, or future reliability.

**Does a missing issue mean the vehicle does not have it?**  
No. Absence from submitted evidence is not proof that a condition does not exist.

**Can a seller claim become a verified fact?**  
Only through a separate, documented verification method appropriate to that exact claim. Repetition or AI rewriting is not verification.

**Assets:** WB-004  
**Current labels:** Seller Check — Pilot workflow planned; ClearSale Marketplace — Coming soon

**Primary CTA:** Follow ClearSale progress  
**Secondary CTA:** Learn the transparency model

## Hero

**Eyebrow:** ClearSale by Drivable · Coming soon

**Headline:** A clearer listing starts with the source of every claim.

**Subhead:** ClearSale is being designed to keep seller statements, submitted observations, verification, conflicts, and unknowns visibly distinct throughout a vehicle listing.

**Primary CTA:** Follow ClearSale progress

**Secondary CTA:** See the transparency model

**Hero qualifier:** ClearSale cannot guarantee honesty, listing completeness, vehicle condition, title status, transaction outcome, or freedom from fraud.

## Product Architecture

**Seller Check** is the planned intake workflow: guided disclosures, requested evidence, documents, observations, and explicit unknowns.

**ClearSale** is the planned marketplace experience: a listing preserves the provenance of those fields instead of flattening them into unqualified promotional language.

## Transparency Model

### Claims remain claims

A seller's statement stays attributed to the seller unless an independent verification method supports it.

### Evidence has scope

A photo can support a limited visible observation. It cannot prove what is outside the frame, authenticate title, establish mechanical condition, or predict future reliability.

### Unknowns stay visible

Missing information should not be silently removed from the listing or converted into a positive statement.

### Conflicts are not averaged away

When sources disagree, the conflict should remain visible until appropriately resolved.

## AI Listing Disclosure

**Heading:** AI can draft the listing. It cannot promote a claim into a fact.

Approved disclosure:

`This listing text was drafted with AI from seller-provided information and submitted evidence. Seller claims remain attributed to the seller. Review the evidence and unknowns before relying on any statement.`

## CTA

**Heading:** Follow the work toward evidence-forward vehicle listings.

**CTA:** Follow ClearSale progress

Do not offer `List now`, accept payment, or imply active marketplace inventory until the workflow, terms, moderation, support, transaction model, and legal review are ready.

## FAQ

**Does ClearSale guarantee that sellers are honest?**  
No. The design goal is to make provenance and unknowns harder to obscure, not to promise that deception is impossible.

**Will Drivable verify every statement?**  
No. Each field must show whether it is seller-stated, observed in submitted evidence, supported by a supplied document, independently verified, unknown, or conflicting.

**Is ClearSale live?**  
No. Seller Check is a planned pilot workflow, and the ClearSale marketplace is coming soon.

## Planned Flow

1. The user chooses to request professional help.
2. With explicit permission, relevant report evidence is selected for sharing.
3. The request is routed according to geography, stated provider capabilities, capacity, and the pilot's operating rules.
4. The provider confirms scope, estimate, credentials, warranty, scheduling, and payment directly with the user.

Do not imply that all evidence is shared automatically or that a report is a work order.

## Better Conversation Guide

Useful questions include:

- What evidence supports this recommended work?
- Is there a diagnostic charge, and what does it cover?
- Which work is urgent, which is preventive, and which can wait?
- Are there alternative tests or repair paths?
- What parts, labor, fees, taxes, and warranty are included in the estimate?
- Will you contact me before exceeding the authorized amount?

Drivable should teach estimate literacy and evidence questions. It should not label ordinary phrases as proof that a mechanic is dishonest.

## Provider Standards Boundary

Do not call participating providers `vetted`, `verified`, or `trusted` until a documented program defines screening, credential review, insurance expectations, complaint handling, re-check frequency, geographic limits, suspension, and audit records.

# E. How Evidence Works — Shared Explainer

**Asset:** WB-007

## Headline

Evidence in. Limits visible.

## Evidence Lifecycle

`Requested → submitted → quality checked → classified by source → reviewed or analyzed → used in a bounded report → optionally reused with permission → corrected, retained, or deleted under policy`

### Requested

The product asks for evidence only when it has a defined purpose and explains safe capture.

### Submitted

The user supplies an observation, media file, device reading, document, or statement. Submission does not establish truth.

### Quality checked

The system checks whether the evidence is readable, relevant, complete enough, and free from obvious conflicts. A quality check is not authenticity verification.

### Classified by source

The item remains labeled as user-stated, seller-stated, observed, device-reported, document-supplied, professionally reported, or independently verified as appropriate.

### Used in a bounded report

The report connects each result to supporting, conflicting, and missing evidence. It does not conceal uncertainty.

### Reused only with permission

Vehicle evidence may support another authorized Drivable workflow, but the user should know what is reused, for what purpose, and who can access it. Marketing permission is separate.

## Confidence Language

- **Higher confidence:** multiple relevant signals align, important conflicts are absent, and remaining unknowns are not material to the limited conclusion.
- **Moderate confidence:** some useful signals align, but missing or conflicting evidence could change the ranking.
- **Low confidence:** available evidence weakly supports a possibility; more evidence or human review is needed.
- **Unable to rank responsibly:** evidence is too limited, unsafe to collect, out of scope, or materially conflicting.

Do not present these labels as universal numeric probabilities unless the scoring system has been validated and the number means what consumers are likely to think it means.

# F. Safety and Limitations Page

**Asset:** WB-008

## Headline

What Drivable can support—and where qualified help is still required.

## Core Limits

- Drivable does not replace a qualified in-person diagnosis or inspection.
- Drivable does not determine whether a vehicle is safe, roadworthy, legally compliant, correctly titled, fairly priced, or reliable in the future.
- A report is limited by the evidence submitted, its quality, its source, and conditions that may not be visible remotely.
- Human review can reduce some uncertainty but is not a physical inspection or guarantee.
- Users must not collect evidence while driving or perform unsafe inspection or repair activity.
- Urgent warning signs require conservative action, not continued evidence collection.

## Escalate Immediately

The product should interrupt ordinary flow and direct the user to stop and seek appropriate help when submitted information suggests possible fire, smoke, fuel leak, severe overheating, loss of braking or steering, wheel/tire structural risk, electrical hazard, carbon monoxide exposure, vehicle instability, collision damage affecting safe operation, or another immediate threat.

This list is illustrative, not exhaustive. Product safety rules and reviewed escalation copy are authoritative.

## Marketplace and Provider Limits

- Seller and buyer workflows cannot guarantee truth, completeness, condition, ownership, title, payment, or transaction outcome.
- Mechanic Match cannot guarantee availability, qualifications, fit, price, diagnosis, work quality, warranty, or result.
- Users should independently verify material information and provider terms.

## Corrections and Support

Provide visible routes for:

- correcting vehicle or account information;
- reporting unsafe or misleading output;
- challenging a provenance label;
- reporting misuse of personal evidence;
- requesting accessibility support;
- obtaining privacy and retention information.

Use verified contact information only when implementing the page.
