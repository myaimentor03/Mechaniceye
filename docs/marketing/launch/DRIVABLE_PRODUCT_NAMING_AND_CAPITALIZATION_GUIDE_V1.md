# Drivable Product Naming and Capitalization Guide V1

## 1. Master Brand

- Consumer-facing name: **Drivable**
- Endorsed master brand: **Drivable by Mechanic's Eye**
- Company/legacy endorsement: **Mechanic's Eye**

Use **Drivable by Mechanic's Eye** on first formal mention in press materials, legal-adjacent documents, partner decks, and major launch pages. Use **Drivable** thereafter when context is clear.

The apostrophe in **Mechanic's Eye** is required. Do not write `Mechanics Eye`, `Mechanics' Eye`, or `Mechanic Eye`.

## 2. Product Names

| Approved name | Function | Current public label |
|---|---|---|
| **Drivable Check** | Evidence-guided vehicle concern intake and confidence-ranked possible causes | Closed beta in preparation |
| **Buyer Check** | Evidence-oriented purchase due diligence showing supported positives, risks, and unknowns | Closed beta in preparation |
| **Mechanic Match** | Provider request and future specialty matching | Manual referral pilot planned |
| **Seller Check** | Seller evidence and disclosure intake | Pilot workflow planned |
| **ClearSale** | Evidence-forward vehicle marketplace concept | Coming soon |
| **Guided Service** | Future guided repair/service education | Future roadmap |

Use title case exactly as shown. Do not insert hyphens, combine words, or add `AI` to a product name unless a formally approved name change occurs.

## 3. Descriptive Phrases

Preferred:

- `confidence-ranked possible causes`
- `evidence-guided vehicle check`
- `supported positives, risks, and unknowns`
- `seller-provided claim`
- `observed in submitted evidence`
- `independently verified`
- `human review recommended`
- `qualified in-person inspection`
- `participating provider`

Restricted or prohibited without substantiation:

- `diagnosis`, `diagnostic machine`, or `tells you exactly what is wrong`
- `safety check`, `safe to drive`, `roadworthy`, or `certified`
- `honest seller`, `guaranteed transparent`, or `fraud-free`
- `vetted mechanic`, `trusted mechanic`, or `best mechanic`
- `verified vehicle`, `clean vehicle`, or `problem-free`
- `saves money`, `prevents unnecessary repairs`, or quantified performance claims

When explaining the founder vision internally, `diagnostic machine` may describe an aspiration only if immediately corrected to the current bounded product behavior. It is not approved public copy.

## 4. AI Language

Preferred:

- `AI-assisted`
- `AI-generated draft reviewed against submitted evidence`
- `AI helps organize evidence and possible causes`

Do not imply that AI observes something it did not receive, authenticates a document without a real verification process, replaces a qualified professional, or converts a seller statement into fact.

For listings, use:

`AI-generated listing draft based on seller-provided information and submitted evidence.`

Never use:

`AI-verified listing` unless a separately defined, audited verification process genuinely supports that exact statement.

## 5. Possessives and Short Forms

- `Drivable's report` is acceptable in ordinary prose.
- Prefer `the Drivable Check report` when a product distinction matters.
- `Check` alone is too ambiguous in public copy.
- Do not abbreviate Drivable Check as `DC` or Buyer Check as `BC` in consumer-facing materials.
- Asset IDs and internal tables may use documented abbreviations.

## 6. Calls to Action

Approved for the current pre-beta stage:

- `Join the beta waitlist`
- `Apply for the closed beta`
- `See how the check works`
- `Learn what evidence helps`
- `Follow the build`

Blocked until the experience is actually available:

- `Diagnose my car now`
- `Check this car now`
- `Find my mechanic`
- `List on ClearSale`
- `Start repair guidance`

CTA language must match the destination and current release label.

## 7. First-Mention Examples

- `Drivable Check, from Drivable by Mechanic's Eye, helps drivers collect useful vehicle evidence and receive confidence-ranked possible causes.`
- `Buyer Check, from Drivable by Mechanic's Eye, helps a shopper distinguish supported positives, risks, seller claims, and unknowns before a purchase.`
- `ClearSale is a planned Drivable marketplace designed to preserve the distinction between seller claims, observations, verification, and unknowns.`

## 8. Enforcement

Every public asset must use the current product name and release label from the repository readiness matrix. A name, status, or promise change must be updated in the canonical product narrative and release matrix before downstream creative is revised.
