# Drivable Launch Studio

This folder is the source of truth for Drivable's staged marketing and advertising program.

The launch studio exists to prevent scattered files, inconsistent claims, duplicate creative, and premature paid advertising. Assets move through defined stages:

`briefed -> drafted -> reviewed -> approved -> produced -> channel-ready -> published -> measured -> retained/retired`

## Current Operating Position

- Consumer-facing brand: **Drivable**.
- Confirmed master brand: **Drivable by Mechanic's Eye**.
- Current launch stage: **pre-beta foundation**.
- Owner-confirmed first-party domain: **https://getdrivable.com/**; the authorized inspection observed an HTTP 301 redirect to canonical **https://www.getdrivable.com/**.
- Live-site launch status: **functional but not ready for coordinated public traffic**; see the reconciliation report for P0/P1 gates.
- Naming clearance gate: active automotive use of **Drivable**/“Drivable AI” at `drivable.app` and active Experian-owned **ClearSale** use at `clear.sale` require qualified review before irreversible public or paid brand production.
- Public mass-launch advertising: **not yet authorized**.
- Safe work now: brand system, evergreen education, product explanations, completed production specifications, organic-content review masters, and non-claim-dependent imagery.
- Blocked until verified beta evidence: testimonials, savings claims, accuracy claims, customer counts, ratings, mechanic-network scale, outcome claims, and scaled paid acquisition.

## Planned Asset Storage

Final media will be stored under `docs/marketing/launch/assets/` using these groups:

- `brand/` — approved marks, lockups, colors, typography, and usage examples.
- `campaigns/` — campaign masters organized by campaign name.
- `social/` — channel-ready organic social assets.
- `paid/` — paid-media masters and platform exports.
- `web/` — landing-page, press-kit, app-store, and sharing imagery.
- `video/` — scripts, shot lists, captions, thumbnails, source, and final exports.
- `audio/` — voiceover, music licenses, sound effects, and final mixes.
- `press/` — founder photos, product stills, boilerplate, fact sheet, and media kit.
- `templates/` — editable source templates and safe-area guides.
- `proof/` — approved testimonial releases and substantiation records; access should be restricted.

Do not place a final asset into a channel folder without a matching manifest entry and approval state.

## Non-Negotiable Marketing Rules

- Never call a possible cause a diagnosis.
- Never state or imply that Drivable declares a vehicle safe to drive.
- Never convert an unverified seller claim into an observed fact.
- Never promise savings, accuracy, repair success, sale outcome, mechanic quality, or title status.
- Never use a customer story, face, vehicle, quote, audio, or private evidence without permission and a documented release.
- Never show a dangerous evidence-capture behavior, including a driver recording while moving.
- Never publish mock or test reports as real customer outcomes.
- Never use fine print to contradict or cure a misleading headline, image, badge, voiceover, or call to action.
- Never claim Drivable is the first, only, or sole product of its kind without robust comparative substantiation and review.
- Keep public and paid identity assets reversible until the Drivable and ClearSale naming risks receive owner/legal direction.
- Never retain human fill-in placeholders in launch copy. Complete the asset from verified source facts or mark it blocked and remove incomplete public copy.
- Paid media requires a working landing page, analytics, privacy/consent review, support capacity, and a stop-loss budget.
