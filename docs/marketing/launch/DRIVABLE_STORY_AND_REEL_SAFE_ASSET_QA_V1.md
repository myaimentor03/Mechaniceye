## Layout and channel review

- Canvas and native export aspect ratio: 1080 x 1920 (9:16).
- Primary brand, headline, body, CTA, and limitation copy are kept away from the outer frame and common interface-overlay areas.
- Essential meaning does not depend on a lower-edge caption, sound, animation, or tiny legal text.
- Text remains part of the master artwork so reviewers can inspect the exact claim and limitation pair before publication.
- Native JPEG simulations were exported at quality 82 to expose likely compression artifacts without pretending to reproduce every platform's proprietary processing.
- Review is visual and editorial; final platform previews are still required in the actual publishing account because interface overlays and cropping vary by placement and device.

## Findings and corrections

1. The first SO-006 render clipped the right edge of “Something changed.” The headline was reduced and reflowed; the corrected PNG and compressed simulation show the complete phrase.
2. The first SO-009 render clipped “check what is missing.” The emphasized line was reduced and reflowed; the corrected PNG and compressed simulation show the complete phrase.
3. SO-006 originally risked implying an available beta application. Its CTA is “Learn about Drivable,” which is truthful without promising an application or enrollment flow.
4. SO-009 uses “Learn about Buyer Check,” not a transaction or listing-review action that the public experience may not fulfill.
5. The Buyer Check design explicitly preserves seller-reported, observed, and unknown categories. It does not turn seller statements into verified facts.
6. The Drivable Check concepts describe written symptoms, timing, dashboard information, recent work, and user-entered OBD information only. They do not claim photo, audio, video, vision, document, or vibration processing.
7. Every asset carries or is paired with a material limitation. None presents Drivable as a diagnosis, inspection, emergency service, repair authorization, or safety clearance.
8. The four SO-001 vertical masters preserve written-observation, uncertainty, no-testing, and no-causation boundaries. They instruct users to park before writing and do not request new driving maneuvers or dangerous evidence collection.
9. The SO-001 vertical examples are explicitly generic educational examples rather than real vehicle reports, and timing is never presented as proof of cause or blame.

## Publication gates

These assets may advance from review only when all applicable gates are evidenced:

- the linked public route and CTA work on desktop and mobile;
- the public copy matches the proven end-to-end product behavior;
- safety escalation and human-review behavior are tested;
- privacy, retention, deletion, consent, and evidence-delivery disclosures are launch-ready;
- capacity and cold-start controls support the planned traffic;
- the owner approves the final creative and naming direction;
- counsel or a qualified trademark professional resolves the known Drivable and ClearSale naming risks before irreversible or paid use;
- final previews are checked inside each intended publishing account immediately before scheduling.

## Review result

All eight vertical masters pass internal visual-boundary, compression-readability, CTA-truthfulness, and claim-safety review. Their status remains **review — not publish-ready**.
