# Drivable Provisional Brand Foundation V1

**Status:** Provisional design system for beta and launch production  
**Confirmed master brand:** Drivable by Mechanic's Eye  
**Rule:** Keep the endorsement line editable and separate from the dominant Drivable wordmark for flexible production.

## 1. Brand Idea

Drivable brings order to the uncertain moment between “something changed” and “I know what to do next.”

The identity should communicate:

- Calm before action.
- Evidence before certainty.
- Technical competence without intimidating jargon.
- Respect for mechanics and hands-on expertise.
- Honest uncertainty rather than artificial confidence.

## 2. Working Brand Architecture

### Consumer Name

**Drivable**

Use the word alone in primary consumer headlines, product navigation, campaign titles, social profiles, and app/store listings.

### Optional Endorsement

**by Mechanic's Eye**

The endorsement appears beneath or beside Drivable as the parent-brand line. It should remain legible but must not compete with the product name.

### Product Family

- Drivable Check.
- Buyer Check.
- Seller Check.
- ClearSale.
- Mechanic Match.

Use title case. Do not use “AI diagnosis,” “AI mechanic,” “Drivable Certified,” or “Mechanic's Eye Approved.”

## 3. Logo Direction

The recommended launch identity is a typography-led wordmark with a small directional/evidence motif—not a literal eyeball, robot, wrench, gear, check-engine icon, or car silhouette.

The mark should be:

- Legible at app-icon and billboard scale.
- Distinct in one color.
- Calm and modern rather than aggressive or racing-oriented.
- Compatible with dark product UI and light marketing surfaces.
- Reproducible as native vector artwork.

Do not finalize or register a mark until naming, trademark screening, domain strategy, and ownership are confirmed.

## 4. Color System

This palette consolidates the strongest existing product-interface colors. It is a working production palette, not a claim that every combination passes accessibility requirements.

| Token | Value | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Midnight 950 | `#07111F` | Primary dark background and serious brand anchor. |
| Midnight 900 | `#0D1A2B` | Secondary dark field. |
| Slate 800 | `#122238` | Cards, panels, dark overlays. |
| Slate 700 | `#223854` | Borders and structural rules. |
| Ice 50 | `#EDF5FF` | Primary text on dark surfaces. |
| Steel 300 | `#9CB0C8` | Secondary dark-surface text; verify size/contrast. |
| Signal Cyan | `#3BD6FF` | Primary action and evidence signal. Use selectively. |
| Clarity Green | `#9AF0C5` | Completed/available/positive process states—not vehicle safety. |
| Caution Amber | `#FFD27D` | Missing information, attention, and caution. |
| Stop Coral | `#FF8F8F` | Stop, block, or critical risk only. Never decorative. |
| Paper | `#F7F9FB` | Light editorial background. |
| Ink | `#172334` | Primary text on light surfaces. |

### Color Behavior

- Cyan means action, evidence, link, or focus—not “AI magic.”
- Green means a process step is available, approved, or complete. It must never imply that a vehicle is safe.
- Amber means attention or missing information, not automatic danger.
- Coral/red is reserved for stop-level or blocked states.
- Safety and confidence must always include text/icon labels; never rely on color alone.

## 5. Typography

### Working Digital Stack

Use **Inter** when it is already available and licensed for the environment, falling back to Arial, Helvetica, and system sans-serif. This aligns with the current UI.

### Production Recommendation

Before public launch, select and license/confirm two families:

- **Primary sans:** humanist or neo-grotesque, high legibility, broad weight range, excellent numerals, strong small-screen rendering.
- **Optional evidence mono:** used sparingly for OBD codes, timestamps, report IDs, or structured evidence labels—not body copy.

Candidate open-source families for design evaluation include Inter, Source Sans 3, IBM Plex Sans, and Public Sans. Final selection requires rendered comparison and license record.

### Type Personality

- Sentence case over excessive all caps.
- Bold, compact headlines without automotive aggression.
- Comfortable line spacing and short readable measures.
- Numerals and codes must remain unambiguous.
- Avoid condensed racing fonts, stencil faces, metallic effects, and fake dashboard typography.

## 6. Layout System

- Use a restrained 8-point spacing system.
- Prefer clear editorial hierarchy: eyebrow, headline, support, CTA, boundary.
- Keep one primary message and one primary CTA per asset.
- Use photographic negative space rather than placing text across faces, phones, dashboards, or vehicle details.
- Use thin structural rules, evidence tags, and calm cards to echo the product without copying the UI literally.
- Corners should be moderately rounded, not toy-like.
- Dark launch assets should include enough light/neutral space to avoid looking like cybersecurity software.

## 7. Image System

### Photography Families

1. **The uncertain moment:** parked driver, dashboard observation, owner reviewing notes.
2. **Evidence in context:** phone, OBD reader, receipt, symptom timeline, vehicle detail.
3. **The better conversation:** owner and mechanic/inspector speaking collaboratively.
4. **Buyer due diligence:** listing, questions, exterior/condition observation, inspection access.
5. **Transparent selling:** honest photos, records, known-issue notes, listing preparation.

### Authenticity Standard

- Everyday vehicles across realistic ages, price bands, body styles, and conditions.
- Diverse adults without tokenized casting or exaggerated emotion.
- Realistic locations: driveway, apartment parking, independent shop, public meeting area, home table.
- Natural light and believable surface wear.
- Screens and documents should be real approved captures or unreadable placeholders—not generated fake product claims.

## 8. Iconography and Illustration

Use simple line icons with consistent stroke, rounded joins, and literal labels. Recommended concepts:

- Evidence/document.
- Written observation / user-entered dashboard or OBD information / missing information.
- Missing information/question.
- Confidence/range.
- Human review.
- Stop/limit/inspect.
- Buyer/seller/mechanic conversation.

Do not use a shield/checkmark to imply certification, safety, or guarantee. Avoid decorative AI circuitry and brain motifs.

## 9. Motion

- Calm transitions and evidence assembly rather than fast glitch effects.
- Use motion to show information becoming organized: separate inputs converging into a clear report structure.
- Safety messages appear immediately and remain readable; never flash by.
- Respect reduced-motion preferences.
- Default social pacing: recognizable situation within 2 seconds, useful idea by 8 seconds, CTA/boundary by 15 seconds.

## 10. Sonic Direction

- Warm, restrained, lightly percussive, and modern.
- Avoid cinematic danger, revving engines, tire squeals, alarm sounds, or “tech reveal” clichés.
- Voiceover should sound like a calm knowledgeable neighbor or consumer advocate—not a salesperson or omniscient mechanic.
- Every music and sound source requires a license record before publication.

## 11. Accessibility Baseline

- Target WCAG 2.2 AA for digital copy and functional graphics.
- Verify actual contrast rather than assuming palette values pass.
- Caption all video accurately and provide transcripts for longer content.
- Do not place essential copy only inside images.
- Supply useful alt text that describes the communication purpose, not every decorative detail.
- Avoid rapid flashing, excessive motion, tiny disclaimer copy, and color-only status encoding.

## 12. Provisional Brand Approval Gate

Before this foundation becomes final:

- Preserve the confirmed Drivable by Mechanic's Eye architecture in all master assets.
- Complete trademark and domain review.
- Approve a vector wordmark and app icon after small-size and one-color tests.
- Confirm type licenses and export strategy.
- Run color contrast checks for the real templates.
- Test the identity with beta users for recognition, trust, and unwanted implications.
- Record final assets, ownership, licenses, and usage rules in the manifest.
