# Drivable Disclaimer Library V1

This library provides working marketing language for **Drivable by Mechanic's Eye**. It is a product and creative control, not legal advice. Final public language requires review for the launch geography, product behavior, insurance position, privacy practices, and distribution channel.

## 1. Universal Boundaries

Use these principles in every channel, even when a full disclaimer will not fit:

- Drivable provides informational support, not a guaranteed diagnosis, inspection, repair, valuation, title determination, or declaration that a vehicle is safe.
- Possible causes must be described as possible, confidence-ranked, and dependent on the evidence supplied.
- Missing or poor-quality evidence must remain visible as uncertainty.
- Seller statements remain attributed claims unless independently observed or verified.
- Safety-critical symptoms require conservative stop-and-escalate language and, where appropriate, human review or an in-person qualified professional.
- Users must not collect photos, audio, video, vibration, text, or OBD information while driving.

## 2. Compact Labels

These labels may appear beside a result, card, image, or claim:

- `Possible cause — not a confirmed diagnosis.`
- `Based on the evidence provided.`
- `More evidence may change this result.`
- `Not a vehicle safety determination.`
- `Seller-provided claim — not independently verified.`
- `Observed in submitted evidence.`
- `Verification not available.`
- `Human review recommended.`
- `Do not capture evidence while driving.`
- `Availability may vary during beta.`

Do not use a compact label to hide a material limitation that belongs next to the headline or call to action.

## 3. Drivable Check

### Short

`Drivable Check identifies up to three possible causes from the evidence you provide. It does not replace an in-person diagnosis or determine whether a vehicle is safe to drive.`

### Standard

`Drivable Check provides informational, confidence-ranked possible causes based on submitted symptoms and evidence. Results are not a confirmed diagnosis, repair instruction, or vehicle safety determination. Stop using the vehicle and contact a qualified professional when warning signs, the report, or your judgment indicate an urgent safety risk.`

### Evidence-capture safety

`Park safely and switch off the vehicle before using your phone. Never record, type, photograph, or connect equipment while driving. Follow the vehicle and equipment manufacturer's instructions.`

### Result-page boundary

`This report reflects the evidence available at the time of review. Missing, unclear, or conflicting evidence may reduce confidence or require human review. A qualified in-person inspection may identify conditions that are not visible here.`

## 4. Buyer Check

### Short

`Buyer Check organizes supported positives, risks, and unknowns. It is not a complete inspection, title guarantee, valuation, or promise of future reliability.`

### Standard

`Buyer Check helps you review submitted vehicle and listing evidence before a purchase. It does not guarantee condition, ownership, title status, value, safety, roadworthiness, or future reliability. Independently verify material facts and obtain an in-person inspection when appropriate.`

### Listing boundary

`Listing statements and seller answers are shown as seller-provided claims unless marked as observed or independently verified. Absence of a reported problem is not proof that no problem exists.`

## 5. Seller Check and ClearSale

### Short

`ClearSale distinguishes seller claims from submitted observations and verification. It does not guarantee that a listing is complete or accurate.`

### Standard

`Seller Check and ClearSale are designed to make evidence, claims, and unknowns easier to distinguish. They do not certify a seller, vehicle, listing, title, price, condition, or transaction. Buyers remain responsible for independent verification and appropriate professional review.`

### AI-generated listing disclosure

`This listing text was drafted with AI from seller-provided information and submitted evidence. Seller claims remain attributed to the seller. Review the evidence and unknowns before relying on any statement.`

### Field provenance labels

- `Seller stated`
- `Observed in submitted evidence`
- `Document supplied — not independently authenticated`
- `Independently verified` — use only when the verification method and reviewer are recorded
- `Unknown / not provided`
- `Conflicting information`

Never change `seller stated` to `observed`, `verified`, `clean`, `good`, or an equivalent factual assertion without documented evidence and approval.

## 6. Mechanic Match

### Before a formal vetting program exists

`Mechanic Match is a planned referral service. Availability, specialties, response times, pricing, and provider participation are not guaranteed.`

### During a manual referral pilot

`Mechanic Match may help route a request to participating providers. Drivable does not guarantee availability, price, diagnosis, workmanship, timing, or outcome. Confirm credentials, scope, estimate, warranty, and payment terms directly with the provider.`

### “Vetted” restriction

Do not use `vetted`, `verified mechanic`, `trusted mechanic`, or an equivalent quality claim until Drivable has a documented screening standard, re-check cadence, complaint process, geographic scope, and auditable provider record. When such a program exists, disclose what the screening does and does not cover.

## 7. Guided Service and Repair Education

`Guided Service is educational support and may not be appropriate for every vehicle, person, tool, location, or repair. Follow manufacturer procedures and local requirements. Stop when instructions, conditions, tools, or personal capability are uncertain, and consult a qualified professional. Never work beneath an unsupported vehicle or disable a safety system.`

This language does not authorize marketing Guided Service before the feature and its safety review are ready.

## 8. Beta and Waitlist

### Waitlist

`Joining the waitlist does not guarantee selection, access date, response time, or continued availability.`

### Closed beta

`This is a limited beta. Features, reports, response times, availability, and eligibility may change. Beta results must not be treated as a final diagnosis, inspection, or safety determination.`

### Feedback permission

`Providing product feedback does not grant Drivable permission to publish your name, likeness, vehicle, evidence, quote, or outcome. Marketing use requires separate, specific permission.`

## 9. Channel Patterns

### Social post or ad

Put the material boundary in the creative or first visible caption lines. A footer link alone is not enough when the headline could otherwise mislead.

Example:

`See possible causes from the evidence you collect — not a confirmed diagnosis or safety determination. Limited beta.`

### Video

- Speak the principal limitation when the narration makes a product promise.
- Keep an on-screen qualifier readable long enough to understand.
- Repeat the capture-safety warning before any collection demonstration.
- Do not rely only on end-card fine print.

### Landing page

- Place the specific limitation near the first product promise and CTA.
- Explain evidence, confidence, unknowns, and human-review behavior before submission.
- Link privacy, consent, terms, safety, and support information before collection.

### Email

Use a short product boundary next to the CTA and link to the relevant limitations page. Do not let subject-line urgency imply a safety conclusion.

### Press or interview

Use the full product category and current release label. Correct any phrasing that calls possible causes diagnoses, calls participating providers vetted, or describes ClearSale as guaranteeing honesty.

## 10. Prohibited Fine-Print Practice

Do not:

- use a disclaimer to contradict a stronger headline;
- put safety-critical limits only behind a link;
- render qualifiers illegibly small, low-contrast, fleeting, or obscured;
- imply certainty through imagery, badges, charts, checkmarks, or voiceover while the text says otherwise;
- publish an approval label that has no recorded approver, scope, date, and supporting evidence;
- imply that account creation, payment, or beta acceptance changes the informational nature of a report.

## 11. Review Record

Before publishing, record:

- asset ID and version;
- product and release stage;
- exact headline, body, CTA, and disclaimer;
- destination page and its matching language;
- geography and channel;
- product owner, safety reviewer, privacy reviewer, and legal reviewer where required;
- approval date, expiration/re-review date, and change notes.
