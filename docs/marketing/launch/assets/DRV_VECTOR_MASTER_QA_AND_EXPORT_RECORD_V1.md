## Design Intent

### Brand Symbol

The symbol uses a D-shaped evidence path with three source/status points and a central clarity line. It intentionally avoids an eyeball, wrench, robot, check-engine light, shield/certification mark, or literal car silhouette.

The color points indicate evidence sources and decision states, not vehicle safety:

- Green: available/completed evidence process.
- Cyan: active evidence/clarity path.
- Amber: attention or unresolved information.

### Typography

Masters use `Inter, Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif`. Inter is preferred when available; final exports must be checked on the actual design workstation. Convert text to outlines only in locked distribution exports, while retaining an editable text master.
