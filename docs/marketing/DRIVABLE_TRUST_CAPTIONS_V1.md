# Drivable Trust Captions V1

## Use

Short captions for posts, ads, carousels, videos, report previews, and landing-page support copy. Keep the applicable safety or inspection context near the caption.

1. Clearer next steps, based on the information you provide.
2. Possible paths, honest confidence, and what is still missing.
3. Drivable helps organize the decision. It does not guarantee the outcome.
4. Better evidence can improve confidence. Missing evidence should lower it.
5. A useful answer can include: "We need more information."
6. Remote guidance is not a substitute for an in-person inspection.
7. Safety-critical questions deserve human review, not automatic reassurance.
8. Drivable explains what may fit the evidence - not what is magically confirmed.
9. Know what to ask before you approve parts.
10. Bring the shop a clearer symptom story.
11. A warning light is one clue, not the whole diagnosis.
12. Do not drive or recreate a symptom just to gather evidence.
13. Stop and seek qualified help when the vehicle feels unsafe.
14. Confidence-rated guidance means uncertainty stays visible.
15. Based on the information provided, with clear limits.
16. Photos, video, audio, codes, and timing can make the next question better.
17. Human review where risk, cost, or evidence requires it.
18. Buying remotely? Review the missing proof before the road trip or deposit.
19. A buyer review is not a pre-purchase inspection.
20. Selling as-is? Be clearer about what you know and what you do not.
21. An as-is listing is not a certification or guarantee.
22. Repair, sell, wait, inspect, or walk away - compare the paths before committing.
23. Drivable cannot promise repair cost, sale price, or future reliability.
24. The safest answer may be to stop and get in-person help.
25. Ask what was tested before replacing another part.
26. Real outcomes matter: what was found, what was repaired, and whether the guidance helped.
27. Customer-reported outcomes and verified repair evidence are not the same thing.
28. Better guidance comes from responsible follow-up, not unsafe certainty.
29. Drivable supports vehicle decisions; it does not replace mechanics, inspectors, towing, or emergency services.
30. Clear information for the decision in front of you.

## Never Pair These Captions With

- "Guaranteed diagnosis"
- "Safe to drive"
- "No mechanic needed"
- "Avoid the tow"
- "We know exactly what is wrong"
- "Guaranteed repair savings"
- "Certified vehicle"
- "Risk-free used car"
- "Guaranteed sale"
