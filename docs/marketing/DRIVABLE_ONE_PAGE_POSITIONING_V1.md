# Drivable One-Page Positioning V1

## One-Sentence Positioning

Drivable helps everyday people make clearer vehicle decisions - drive no farther, stop, tow, inspect, fix, sell, buy, wait, or walk away - with confidence-rated guidance based on the information they provide.

## Customer Problem

Vehicle decisions arrive before certainty does. People hear a noise, see a warning light, face a repair estimate, find a cheap used car, or need to sell a problem vehicle. They often do not know:

- What information matters.
- Whether the situation needs urgent in-person help.
- What evidence to gather safely.
- Which possible paths fit the evidence.
- What to ask a mechanic or seller.
- Whether another repair, inspection, or purchase is worth pursuing.

Search results and generic AI answers can sound certain without understanding the specific case. Shops may receive incomplete symptom descriptions. Buyers and sellers may act before they know what proof is missing.

## Drivable Solution

Drivable turns a messy vehicle question into a structured decision path:

1. Understand the customer's goal and vehicle situation.
2. Capture symptoms, timing, warning lights, history, and safe evidence.
3. Organize possible causes and missing evidence.
4. Rate confidence and identify safety or cost risk.
5. Apply human review where the case requires it.
6. Deliver practical next checks, decision paths, and a mechanic or seller script.
7. Follow up on what the customer did and what actually happened.

## First Target Markets

- Owners with a current vehicle problem.
- Owners deciding whether to repair or sell.
- Used-car buyers deciding whether a listing is worth pursuing.
- Sellers preparing a clearer as-is listing.
- Owners of sitting, dead, or no-start vehicles.
- People who need help preparing for a mechanic conversation.

## Why Now

- Vehicle ownership and repair costs create higher-stakes decisions.
- Used-car listings are often incomplete or difficult to evaluate remotely.
- Customers already use search and AI but need stronger evidence, confidence, and safety boundaries.
- Phones make it easier to collect photos, video, audio, codes, listings, and records.
- Human review can be reserved for higher-risk or lower-confidence cases.
- Outcome capture can improve future questions and confidence calibration responsibly.

## How It Works

**Submit:** Vehicle details, the decision, symptoms, and safe evidence.

**Review:** Drivable organizes possible paths, supporting evidence, missing evidence, confidence, and safety flags.

**Decide:** The customer receives a clearer next move and useful questions for a mechanic, seller, buyer, or inspector.

**Follow up:** The real action, repair, cause, cost, and usefulness are captured when known.

## Trust and Safety Boundaries

Drivable:

- Provides informational, confidence-rated guidance.
- Uses possible-cause and evidence language.
- States what is missing and when confidence is limited.
- Requires human review for high-risk cases.
- Recommends qualified in-person help when needed.

Drivable does not:

- Certify or inspect a vehicle.
- Confirm a diagnosis from incomplete remote evidence.
- Claim a vehicle is safe to drive.
- Guarantee a repair, price, sale, buyer, purchase, title, or legal outcome.
- Replace emergency, towing, mechanic, inspection, or legal services.

## Data Moat

The long-term advantage is not a pile of symptom text. It is a permissioned, structured link between:

- What the customer reported.
- What evidence existed.
- What Drivable and a reviewer recommended.
- The confidence and safety limits at that moment.
- What the customer did.
- What inspection or repair later found.
- What it cost.
- Whether the guidance helped.

That loop can improve questions, evidence requests, escalation, and confidence calibration without pretending every stored case proves learning.

## Launch Wedge

Start locally with one trusted tester and a narrow set of non-emergency decisions. The strongest early wedge is:

> "Before you approve a repair, chase a used car, or give up on the vehicle, organize what you know and get a clearer next move."

Use `First Look Report` as the simplest early paid candidate after the controlled tester flow works. Expand only when safety, review, delivery, and outcome capture remain reliable.
