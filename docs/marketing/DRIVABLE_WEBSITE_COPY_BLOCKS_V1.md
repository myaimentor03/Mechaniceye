## How It Works

### 1. Tell Drivable the decision

Are you dealing with a current problem, buying, selling, checking vehicle health, or deciding what to do with a sitting vehicle?

### 2. Add the useful evidence

Share vehicle details, symptoms, timing, warning lights, history, and photos, video, audio, scan information, or records you can gather safely.

### 3. Get a confidence-rated next step

See possible cause paths, supporting evidence, missing evidence, safety flags, next checks, and useful questions for a mechanic, seller, buyer, or inspector.

### 4. Tell us what happened

When possible, report the action taken, actual cause, repair, cost, and whether the guidance helped. Outcomes can improve future questions and confidence responsibly.

## Trust and Safety

### Trust Block

Drivable should make uncertainty easier to understand, not hide it. Guidance is based on the information provided and should show possible causes, supporting evidence, missing evidence, confidence, and the next useful check.

### Human Review Block

Higher-risk safety, title, structural, high-cost, conflicting, or low-confidence cases may require human review before customer-facing guidance is approved.

### In-Person Help Block

Some vehicle questions cannot be answered responsibly from remote information. Hands-on inspection, testing, towing, emergency response, title verification, or legal help may be required.

### No-Guarantee Block

## FAQ Snippets

### Does Drivable diagnose my vehicle?

Drivable provides informational, confidence-rated possible paths based on the information provided. A confirmed diagnosis may require qualified hands-on inspection and testing.

### Can Drivable tell me it is safe to drive?

No. Drivable can identify risk signals and conservative next steps, but it does not provide safety clearance. If the vehicle feels unsafe or has critical symptoms, stop and seek qualified help.

### What evidence helps?

Vehicle details, symptom timing, exact warning lights, recent repairs, safe photos or video, audio, scan codes, listing screenshots, and records can help. Do not take a safety risk to collect evidence.

### What if there is not enough information?

The responsible result may be a missing-information request, lower confidence, human review, or a recommendation for in-person inspection.

### Can this replace a pre-purchase inspection?

No. A Buyer Remote Risk Review can help identify red flags, missing proof, seller questions, and inspection priorities, but it does not inspect or verify the vehicle.

### Does Drivable guarantee I will save money?

No. Drivable helps organize the decision and questions. It cannot guarantee repair savings, repair success, sale price, or financial outcome.

### How does Drivable improve?

With appropriate consent and controls, follow-up can connect the original evidence and recommendation to the customer's action and actual outcome. Customer reports and verified repair evidence remain distinct.

### Is human review available?

Human review is planned for cases where risk, cost, conflicting evidence, or low confidence requires another layer. Availability and turnaround must be defined before it is promised.
