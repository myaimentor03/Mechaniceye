## Severity Definitions

- `Blocker`: Testing or launch must stop; safety, data loss, wrong recipient, broken build, or unusable core flow.
- `High`: Major route, report, trust, or workflow failure with no acceptable workaround.
- `Medium`: Important defect or confusion with a workable temporary path.
- `Low`: Limited-impact issue that does not prevent the controlled test.
- `Polish`: Copy, spacing, visual, or convenience improvement with no material workflow risk.
